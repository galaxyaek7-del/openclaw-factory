# The Etsy GPSR Fields, Explained Line by Line (What I Learned Researching EU Compliance)

*Disclosure: I make compliance toolkits for sellers, including a GPSR Action Kit linked at the end. Everything below stands alone — you don't need to buy anything to use it.*

If you sell on Etsy to EU buyers, you've seen the "EU product compliance" section in the listing editor. Here's what each field actually wants, in plain language.

**1. Trader status.** If you're a registered business shipping to the EEA, Etsy generally designates you a trader. Check your shop settings rather than assuming — the fields you see depend on it.

**2. Manufacturer block.** Name/trade name plus postal AND electronic contact. If you make it, that's you. If a production partner makes it, determine the legal manufacturer first — a partner is not automatically the manufacturer, and guessing wrong here invalidates everything downstream.

**3. Responsible Person block.** For non-EU sellers, this is an EU-established party named on your behalf, with name, postal address, and electronic contact. Copy-paste from your mandate documents — never retype from memory. Transcription typos are a common, fixable reason listings get flagged.

**4. Identifiers.** Picture, product type, plus one more identifier (SKU, batch, model). Keep them identical across your listing, your label, and your records. Mismatches are among the first things reviewers check.

**5. Safety information.** Warnings in understandable language, traceable to a real assessment of your product. Short and standard beats long and invented.

**The three most commonly flagged mistakes:** blank Responsible Person fields (listings go invisible in EU search), names that differ between listing and label, and safety text that contradicts the product photos.

**What no listing field can do:** the fields are the visible tip. Behind them, the regulation expects a technical file and risk assessment per product line, kept for ten years. If an authority ever asks, screenshots of your listings won't answer — the file does. Build it once, reuse it per product.

**15-minute fix routine (do this today):** (1) open your top EU-visible listing, (2) paste Responsible Person data from your mandate, (3) unify one identifier across listing and label, (4) shorten safety text to what you can defend, (5) screenshot the finished section with today's date. Repeat per listing. Free checklist version of this routine: trader check → manufacturer block → RP paste → identifiers → safety text → screenshot filed.
