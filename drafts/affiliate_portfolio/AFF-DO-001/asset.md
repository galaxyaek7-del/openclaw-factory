## The check most affiliate guides skip

A 10% recurring commission looks attractive. A payout that cannot reach your account is worth zero. Before you write a single post, verify the payment path end to end:

1. **Program eligibility.** Does DigitalOcean accept publishers in your country, and does it accept the content and traffic source you plan to use?
2. **Network account.** Can you open and verify an Awin publisher account from Algeria?
3. **Receiving method.** Is Payoneer available to you, and can Awin send USD to it?
4. **Identity and tax.** Can you complete the required tax documentation and any identity checks without a document you do not have? The exact form is not confirmed on the reviewed pages.
5. **Threshold and fees.** Is the USD 20 minimum reachable, and what does the 1.75% conversion fee or SWIFT fee leave after conversion?
6. **Payment timing.** Are the 1st and 15th realistic for your cash flow, and how long does the network hold a commission before it becomes payable?
7. **Attribution.** Do you have a real tracking link, and can you see clicks and approved conversions in the network report?

If any answer is "I think so," it is not verified yet.

## What the record does not prove

This research does not prove:

- that your application will be approved;
- that every Algeria-based publisher can use the same payout route;
- that a referral will convert into a paying customer;
- that a commission will be approved, paid, or retained;
- that a 30-day cookie is the only attribution path;
- that the terms will stay the same.

It also does not prove demand. A program page describes an offer, not a market. Demand requires real behavior: qualified visits, outbound clicks, approved conversions, and eventually paid commissions.

## A decision rule that keeps you honest

Use this sequence:

```text
verified payout path
  -> approved program account
  -> real tracking link
  -> one specific reader with one specific problem
  -> measured outbound clicks
  -> approved conversions
  -> confirmed commission
  -> received payout
```

Do not reorder it. Do not write "earn passive income" because a program exists. Do not treat a click as a sale. Do not treat a pending commission as revenue.
