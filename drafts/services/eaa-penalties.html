What can a fine be under the European Accessibility Act? Read the statute, not the sales page.

Since 28 June 2025 the EAA applies to e-commerce services offered to EU consumers. Each member state sets its own penalties, and the tables vendors publish disagree with each other, and sometimes with the law they cite.
