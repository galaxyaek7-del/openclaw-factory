Which of your n8n workflows stopped working without telling you?

An automation that fails rarely announces it. Ours did: every company workflow on our own n8n failed for twelve days while the health check said OK, because the server still answered. This audit looks at what actually ran.

What you get

Every workflow on your instance labelled OK, FAILING, STALE, INACTIVE or UNKNOWN, with the reason, and a short list of what needs attention first. A workflow is only called OK after a recent successful run on the schedule you tell us it should keep.

What we need, and what we never touch

A read-only API key you create and delete afterwards (or the two JSON exports of your workflows and executions, without execution data), and how often each workflow should run. We never read your database, credentials or the data your workflows carry, and we change nothing on your instance.

Not included

Fixing workflows, Zapier or Make, uptime monitoring, and whether a "successful" run did the right thing in your business: that question comes back to you.
