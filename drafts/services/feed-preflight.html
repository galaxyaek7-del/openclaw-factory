Find the feed errors before Google disapproves your products

A disapproved item disappears from Shopping ads and free listings until it is fixed and reviewed again. Most fixes happen after the damage. This check runs on your exported feed file (CSV, TSV or XML) before you upload it, and needs no access to your Merchant Center account.

What it checks

Only rules published in Google's own product data specification and GTIN rules: required columns and values, the price format (a point for decimals and a currency code), availability values, title and ID length, duplicate IDs, links without http(s), the GTIN check digit and restricted GS1 ranges, and an identifier flag that contradicts a GTIN. It never invents a code: a wrong GTIN is reported for you to confirm with the manufacturer.

What it does not check

Whether your feed price matches the landing page and checkout, image and policy review, category-specific attributes, and account settings such as shipping and tax: Google decides those at review. No approval is promised.
