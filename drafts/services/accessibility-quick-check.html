A first accessibility fix list for a small shop

If your site has never had an accessibility review, start with what a machine can find reliably: images without alternative text, form fields with no label, links and buttons with no name, a page with no language or title, zoom switched off. Each finding comes with the page, the line and the WCAG 2.2 criterion it fails.

What this is not. It is not an audit, not a certification and not evidence of WCAG, ADA or European Accessibility Act compliance. Keyboard use, colour contrast, screen-reader behaviour and whether alt text actually describes the image all need a person, and every report lists them as not checked. In 2025 the US FTC ordered an accessibility vendor to pay $1M for claiming its automated tool made sites compliant; we make no such claim.
