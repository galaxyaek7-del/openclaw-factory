## 1) Notion for Lawyers / Solo Law Firms — RECOMMENDED FIRST

**Evidence found (real, not assumed):**
- Notion Marketplace: **231 templates** in category "Legal Services" (https://www.notion.com/templates/category/legal-services) — proves supply and interest, but also competition in the broad "legal" bucket
- Dedicated guides: Landmark Labs "Notion for Legal Case Management: Ultimate Guide (2024)" (case files, research repository, trial prep, relational databases, calendar) — https://www.landmarklabs.co/blog/notion-for-legal-case-management-ultimate-guide-2024
- Notion for Lawyers complete guide 2025: https://templatesfornotion.com/post/notion-for-lawyers-complete-guide-2025
- LexRatio 2025-01-15: "How Notion Can Be Used as a Legal Tech Solution" — case tracking, deadlines, meeting notes, knowledge hub
- 18 free guides at https://notionforlawyers.com/guides (Raghav R Handa) — client intake, matter management, billable hours, conflict checks
- Templates: Legal Practice Management OS by Younes, LegalPro Manager — both for law firms/solo attorneys

**Why micro, not generic "Notion for everyone":**
- Generic "Notion review" competes with thousands of affiliates and Notion's own site. "Notion for lawyers" narrows to ~231 templates vs 1000s broadly, and the guides above show real but fragmented competition — opportunity to own "Notion for solo law firms" with specific workflow (case → client → deadline → billing), not just template list.

**Competition in this slice:** 231 Legal Services templates is significant, but most are single templates; none of the top results we found is a comprehensive, evidence-backed comparison + guide + template series with affiliate disclosure handled correctly. Gap: honest, drawback-including content with Founder personal input.

**Buyer type:** Solo attorney, 2-10 person law firm, paralegal — identifiable via legal industry directories, not general consumers

**Content angle to reduce competition:** Focus on "Notion for solo law firms: client intake → matter → billable hours → deadline" workflow (the 18 guides show this is the real pain, not generic note-taking), with a free Notion template for that exact workflow, not a generic Notion review.

**Honest limitation:** Notion is not a replacement for Clio/MyCase for compliance-heavy firms (noted in Landmark Labs FAQ and LexRatio). Any content must include this drawback.

**Evidence found:**

- Cleverence: "Best Inventory Apps for Distributors and Wholesalers (2025 Guide)" — different but confirms B2B inventory need (tangential)

**Why micro, not generic:** Same logic — 176 templates is fewer than 231 legal, and the xPM evidence shows construction professionals do pay for Notion templates ($49-$149 one-time), proving willingness to pay in this slice. Generic "Notion project management" has 1000s of templates; "Notion for construction project management" with pre-construction checklists, budget tracking, subcontractor hub is a tighter slice.

**Competition in this slice:** 176 + 93 free = moderate. Top Gillde post is a listicle; opportunity to do deeper: detailed review of 1-2 construction templates (e.g., Construction Project OS) vs competitor, plus a free Notion template for "pre-construction checklist" for a specific buyer (e.g., small contractor).

**Buyer type:** Solo contractor, small construction firm, project manager — identifiable via construction industry channels

**Content angle:** "Notion for small construction contractors: pre-construction checklists → budget tracking → subcontractor management" — specific workflow, not generic project management.

**Honest limitation:** Notion is not a replacement for Procore/Buildertrend for large firms; must include as drawback.
