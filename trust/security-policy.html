Security Policy

Last reviewed: July 23, 2026

We're a small, early-stage business, not a security team of hundreds — but we treat security as a real discipline, not an afterthought. Here's how, honestly.

What we build

  • Administrative access to our own systems requires authentication, with signed sessions verified using constant-time comparison (to resist timing-based attacks) and a fixed session lifetime.
  • We run automated dependency scanning and keep our software dependencies current against known vulnerabilities.
  • Our systems undergo regular internal security review — checking for injection vulnerabilities, unsafe code patterns, and unauthorized access paths — and real findings get fixed, not just logged.
  • We don't store payment card details, ever. Every purchase is processed by the platform you buy through (Paddle, Gumroad, Etsy, or Amazon KDP) under their own security and compliance programs.

What we don't do (yet), stated plainly

  • We do not currently hold a formal third-party security certification (such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001). These require an ongoing compliance program that isn't justified at our current size — we'll pursue this if and when we have the customer base to warrant it.
  • We have not commissioned a professional penetration test. Our own internal review process is real, but it isn't a substitute for one, and we don't claim it is.

Reporting a security issue

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability in any Galaxy Forge product or system, please email galaxyaek7@gmail.com with details. We read every message ourselves, and we'll acknowledge real reports promptly. Please give us a reasonable window to investigate and fix an issue before disclosing it publicly.

Changes to this policy

As this business grows, this policy will be updated to reflect what's actually true at the time — we'd rather under-claim than over-promise.
