Refunds go through the platform you bought from first

Because every purchase is processed by Amazon KDP, Etsy, Gumroad, or Paddle — not directly by us — each platform's own refund process is the fastest and most reliable way to request one, and each has its own rules and windows that legally apply to your purchase:

  • Amazon KDP: use Amazon's own content return process for your order.
  • Etsy: contact the shop (us) through Etsy's messaging system, or use Etsy's own buyer protection process if applicable.
  • Gumroad: use Gumroad's receipt link, or contact us directly.
  • Paddle: use the link in your Paddle receipt email, or contact us directly.

Our own stance, on top of the platform's policy

If a product is genuinely defective — the file doesn't open, pages are missing or corrupted, or it clearly doesn't match its description — email us and we'll make it right: a replacement file or a full refund, your choice. We're not going to hide behind a platform's fine print for a real mistake on our end.
