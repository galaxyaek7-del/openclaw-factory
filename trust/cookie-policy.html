Cookie Policy

Draft prepared: 2026-08-09
This is a draft, written to accurately reflect real current practice — it has not been reviewed by a lawyer and should not be treated as final or legally binding until it has. The specific consent requirements that apply to you depend on your jurisdiction, which hasn't been confirmed yet.

The short version

We use exactly one real cookie, and only if you create an account and log in. We do not run any analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies on any page.

What we track without a cookie

We record real, anonymous page-view and click events (which page was viewed, which solution link was clicked, when) to understand what's actually useful — this is a server-side event log, not a cookie, and it isn't tied to you personally unless you're logged in and take an action that's naturally associated with your account (like submitting a support ticket).

What we don't use

  • No Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any third-party analytics/advertising script.
  • No cross-site tracking or ad-retargeting cookies.
  • No cookie banner consent-manager third-party script (we have nothing non-essential to ask consent for today — if that changes, this page will be updated first, not after).

Third-party links

Some links on this site go to partner/affiliate programs (see our Affiliate Disclosure). Once you leave our site, that destination's own cookie policy applies — we have no control over it.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser at any time. Doing so will only affect whether you stay logged in — it won't break any other page on this site, since nothing else here depends on a cookie.
