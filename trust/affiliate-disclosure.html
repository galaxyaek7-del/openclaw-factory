Affiliate Disclosure

Last reviewed: 2026-08-09
Some links on Galaxy Forge are partner links. If you purchase or sign up through one of these links, we may receive a commission at no additional cost to you.

What this means in practice

Some of the solutions we list are affiliate or referral partnerships. If you choose one of these through a link on our site, the vendor may pay us a commission. This never changes the price you pay.

How this does NOT affect what we show you

This is the part most disclosures don't explain, so here it is plainly:

  1. A solution is only listed if it clears a real evidence check first — its commission terms have to be independently confirmed against the vendor's own official page. If we can't verify that, it isn't shown, no matter how much it might pay.
  2. Ranking never uses commission size as an input. Our public catalog is ordered by verification strength and evidence freshness — the same real signal order we use internally — never by how much a program pays per referral.
  3. We show solutions with no real commission relationship too, when they're the right evidence-backed answer to a real problem.

Why we do it this way

A recommendation that's secretly optimized for commission isn't a recommendation — it's an ad wearing a disguise. We'd rather be a company you trust to actually help you pick the right thing, even when that costs us a possible commission on a page.

What we don't do

  • We don't accept payment from a vendor to be ranked higher.
  • We don't write fake reviews or fake testimonials for a solution to make it look better.
  • We don't manufacture urgency or scarcity to push you toward a partner link.
  • We don't hide which links are partner links — every one on this site is disclosed on the page it appears on.
