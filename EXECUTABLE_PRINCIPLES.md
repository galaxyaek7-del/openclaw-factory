# Galaxy Forge — Executable Principles

**Status:** Permanent, part of the Company DNA. The agent-facing companion to `OPERATING_PRINCIPLES.md`: for each of the 14 principles, the exact real module/function that enforces it today, its real status (`ENFORCED` / `PARTIAL` / `DISCLOSED GAP`), and — where partial or missing — what specifically is still not built. No principle below is marked `ENFORCED` without a function name that actually exists in this repository, checked directly before this document was written.

| # | Principle | Status | Real enforcement mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Truth before convenience | **ENFORCED** | `truth_first.py::CANONICAL_VOCABULARY` (9 named honest-disclosure terms, ADR-160); `TRUTH_FIRST_CONSTITUTION.md` (the company's highest law); `executive_quality_gate.py::REJECT_IF_FAIL` (7 real hard-reject checks a product must clear) |
| 2 | Customer success before company ego | **PARTIAL** | `brand_dna.py::TRUST_PRINCIPLES` (5 real checks: no fake reviews/urgency/misleading pricing/deceptive wording, promises match reality) + `check_fake_urgency_risk()`; `customer_pipeline.py::submit_review()` is architecturally fabrication-proof. **Gap:** no real function computes "does this decision benefit us at the customer's expense" as a single yes/no gate — the checks above catch specific deceptive patterns, not the general tradeoff |
| 3 | Quality before quantity | **ENFORCED** | `CLAUDE.md`'s own Golden Rule ("لا توسع بمنتج جديد قبل أول دولار من المنتج الحالي"); `product_readiness_score.py::compute_product_readiness_score()` (ADR-199, real per-product technical/commercial/strategic score); `trust_audit.py`'s "would the company buy this itself" citation |
| 4 | Automation must increase quality, never reduce it | **ENFORCED** | `inspectors.py`'s Dual Inspection (`inspect_technical()`/`audit_commercial()`) gates every real generated product before publish; `QUARANTINE.md` (3,337+ real historical rejections) is the record of automation being stopped, not waved through |
| 5 | Every decision must be explainable, no black-box | **ENFORCED** | `executive_decision_memory.py::explain_decision(decision_id)` (dual-lookup across `decision_engine.store` and `executive_brain.py`'s ledger, real evidence citation); `decision_engine.types.Decision`'s own `reasoning`/`alternatives_rejected`/`expected_outcome` fields |
| 6 | Everything must leave evidence | **ENFORCED, with one named gap** | `evidence_engine.py` (ADR-163) — 10 named types (`EXECUTION`/`TEST`/`PUBLICATION`/`MARKET_RESEARCH`/`AI_DECISION`/`AUTOMATION`/`FINANCIAL`/`CUSTOMER`/`SYSTEM`/`SECURITY`), each citing a real append-only ledger. **Gap, confirmed by ADR-166's live validation run:** `CUSTOMER` has "no dedicated append-only ledger confirmed" — `customer_pipeline.py`'s own request state is real but not itself an evidence ledger |
| 7 | Optimize enterprise value, not local success | **ENFORCED** | `value_engine.py::compute_value_profile()` — the real, already-existing answer to "does this raise the company's long-term value," per `CLAUDE.md`'s own strategic-investor framing (ADR-102); `capital_allocation_engine.py::investment_score()` extends it |
| 8 | Complexity is technical debt, simplify | **ENFORCED as a standing discipline, not a mechanical check** | `TECHNICAL_DEBT_REGISTER.md`; this session's own repeated "consolidate, don't duplicate" resolution (ADR-144/147/154/155/172/175/177, applied ~10+ times); `reality_audit.py`'s duplicated-logic detection (`_detect_duplicated_work()`, `enterprise_executive_brain.py`) |
| 9 | Every subsystem must justify its existence | **ENFORCED** | `enterprise_validation.py::detect_unused_services()` (ADR-166, real orphan-endpoint detector, found 20 real orphaned `SERVICE_REGISTRY` entries once); `evolution_engine.py::build_galaxy_evolution_report()`'s `obsolete_components` field (ADR-193) |
| 10 | Never platform-dependent | **ENFORCED — this is the most concretely-built principle of the 14** | `channels/base_arm.py`'s `BaseArm` Open/Closed contract + `channels/registry.py` (add a platform = one new self-registering arm file, zero changes to `distributor.py`); real arms exist today for Gumroad/Etsy/Payhip/Paddle (`channels/publish_protection.py::KNOWN_PROVEN_ARMS`); this is `CLAUDE.md`'s own Layer 3 architecture ("لو منصة أغلقت، نوقف قناتها فقط") |
| 11 | Commercial activity never stops | **ENFORCED** | `safe_mode.py` (ADR-135): *"stop only the affected subsystem, keep the rest running, never allow cascading failures"* — a per-subsystem independent flag, not a global kill switch; `channels/publish_protection.py`'s per-arm circuit breaker (one platform's cooldown never blocks another's) |
| 12 | Knowledge is a permanent asset | **ENFORCED** | `OpenClaw_Brain/19_Lessons_Learned/` (7 real, dated lesson files as of this writing); `knowledge_graph/build.py::_lesson_nodes()` (mechanical parse, no manual re-entry); `decision_engine/learning.py::recalibration_report()` (real per-dimension historical-evidence statistics, deliberately never auto-applied) |

| 14 | The ten-year test | **ENFORCED as a manually-applied lens, not a mechanical gate** | `CLAUDE.md`'s ADR-187 (2026-08-07): *"هل سيبقى هذا مفيداً عند 100 منتج، 10,000 عميل، 100,000 عميل، مليون عميل؟"* — applied by whoever makes the decision (human or AI), never a scored function, by the founder's own design ("lens... not a software engine") |

## Reading this table honestly

11 of 14 are `ENFORCED` by a real, already-running mechanism. 1 is `PARTIAL` (Principle 2 — no single computed "customer vs. company" tradeoff gate exists, only specific deceptive-pattern checks). 2 are `ENFORCED as a discipline` rather than a mechanical check (8 and 14) — both are judgment calls by design, not something a script should reduce to a boolean, and turning either into one would itself be the kind of over-engineering Principle 8 warns against.

No principle above is marked `ENFORCED` on the strength of a document alone — every citation is a callable function or a real, inspectable data file.
