# Clinic No-Show Reduction Kit - Value Notes

## Positioning
For dental and medical outpatient practices losing chair and room time to no-shows. The kit installs a 3-touch confirmation routine with waitlist backfill, run by the existing front desk with tools they already have.

## Primary buyer
Practice owner or office manager who owns the schedule and feels the 15-20 percent no-show range directly in weekly revenue.

## Core promise (operational, not outcome-based)
A documented daily routine: confirm at 7 days, remind at 48 hours, remind same-day, refill every open slot from a live waitlist, and track reasons so patterns show up in the practice's own numbers.

## Why it fits
- Three touches instead of one call that reaches about 60 percent of patients.
- YES/NO replies make confirmations countable and NO replies actionable the same day.
- Waitlist log turns cancellations into refilled slots within hours.
- Reason codes reveal whether the problem is forgetfulness, transport, cost questions, or scheduling conflicts.
- 90-day rollout moves in phases: baseline, pilot with one provider, expand, review.

## Price logic
$59 one-time. A single refilled appointment can cover many times the price; the kit itself promises no specific result.

## Differentiation
Administrative only, no clinical claims, no software to install. Works with manual sending or an existing reminder system. Privacy-safe logs use reference codes, never patient names.

## Limits stated openly
Scheduling workflow only. No clinical guidance, no staffing or payer advice, no software included. All worked examples are labeled illustrative.
