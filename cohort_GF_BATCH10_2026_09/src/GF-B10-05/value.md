# Freelancer Scope Control - Value Notes

Positioning: written boundaries for solo earners. The product sells calm, specific language at the moment scope tries to expand.

Primary buyer: freelancer on fixed-price or hourly work who scopes and invoices alone.

Core promise (without outcome promises): every engagement gets numbered deliverables, written exclusions, mapped milestones, counted revisions, and priced changes.

Differentiation:
- Exclusions written into the SOW, not added later.
- Milestone-to-deliverable mapping so payment ties to defined outputs.
- 15-minute courtesy rule with a log, replacing informal favors.
- Say-yes-and-quote scripts that keep tone cooperative while stating fee and date.

Price $39: positioned as less than the cost of one unbilled revision round (illustrative comparison). Pays for itself when one change order is priced instead of absorbed.

Use moments: new client onboarding, mid-project extra request, revision round 3, unfunded milestone, rush ask.

Non-goals: legal drafting for a specific jurisdiction, dispute resolution, collection, agency workflows.
