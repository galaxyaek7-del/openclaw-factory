# PCI SAQ Readiness Kit - Value Notes

## Positioning
For US small merchants who dread the annual card-compliance paperwork. The kit turns a confusing questionnaire into an orderly file: candidate path, scope list, evidence log, incident plan, renewal dates.

## Primary buyer
Owner or office manager at a retail, food, or services business taking card payments. No security background assumed.

## Core promise (operational, not outcome-based)
An organized readiness file you can walk through with your acquirer, maintained on a calendar instead of rebuilt in a panic each year.

## Why it fits
- Starts with the decision tree, so effort goes to the right path from day one.
- Scope-reduction planner attacks the costliest problem: drift toward the heavy SAQ D path.
- Readiness log with status formulas shows what is done and what is missing at a glance.
- Incident plan and provider list close two common gaps: MFA-adjacent access control and missing response steps.
- Renewal calendar keeps attestation, provider reviews, and plan tests on schedule.

## Price logic
$59 one-time. Less than one hour of outside help, reusable every year across the whole attestation cycle.

## Differentiation
Plain language for non-technical owners. Every tool maps to a real acquirer conversation. Disclosure is printed in the guide, the templates, and the listing: no compliance guarantees; your acquirer/bank decides what is required.

## Limits stated openly
Does not select the final SAQ, does not validate controls, does not replace acquirer or assessor advice. Illustrative examples are teaching cases.
