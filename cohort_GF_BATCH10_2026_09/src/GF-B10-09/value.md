# Handbook Acknowledgment Tracker - Value Notes

Price: $49. Audience: US SMBs with 5 to 200 employees and no HR department.

Core tension: the handbook exists but the proof does not. Owners carry the risk of the nobody told me defense with only email threads and scattered PDFs to answer it.

Positioning: a calm administrative record. One current version, every acknowledgment dated, every gap assigned a next date. Built for the owner or office manager who does HR tasks without an HR title.

What makes it usable:
- Version register stops silent edits and version confusion.
- Formula-driven log shows signed, missing, and overdue counts without hand counting.
- Chase list turns nagging into a dated routine with stages and channels.
- Witnessed refusal form converts a tense moment into a factual note.
- Annual calendar keeps review on schedule outside peak season.

Boundaries stated plainly: process tool, not legal advice; confirm e-signature and retention practice with counsel. No claims about dispute results. No drafting of policies.

Fit check: strong fit for 5 to 200 employees with one or two administrators. Not a fit for dedicated HR teams or complex multi-state setups needing automated systems.

Use promise kept small: steady entries, weekly reviews during windows, archived cycles. Reliability compounds through routine.
