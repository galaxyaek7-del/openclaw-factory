# Food Truck Permit Navigator - Value Notes

Price: $59. Audience: first-time US food-truck operators, pre-launch.

Core tension: excitement about food meets confusion about paperwork. Five to twelve items across offices that never talk to each other, totals in the low thousands, 4 to 10 weeks of calendar, one missing letter stalling everything.

Positioning: a calm path through fragmented offices. Map what applies, verify each fee and form at the source, file in dependency order, track every filing with a next date, arrive inspected and stay current.

What makes it usable:
- Permit map turns unknown unknowns into yes, no, or verify rows.
- Estimator separates illustrative ranges for learning from verified fees for planning, with formula sums.
- Dependency order (entity, certifications, commissary, inspections, vendor permit) prevents reject-and-requeue loops.
- Tracker plus packet checklist keeps filings complete and follow-up dated.
- Renewal calendar with alert-date formulas plus reminder setup prevents expiration shutdowns.

Boundaries stated plainly: planning information, not legal/tax advice; verify every fee and form with the issuing agency. Illustrative ranges only, never current facts. No promises on approvals or dates.

Fit check: strong fit for pre-launch solo operators entering one or two cities. Not a fit for those wanting confirmed fee schedules or legal advice inside the kit.

Use promise kept small: verify, file complete packets, log dates same day, review calendar monthly. Readiness compounds through dated routine.
