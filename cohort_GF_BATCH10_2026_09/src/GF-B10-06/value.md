# DSAR Response Kit - Value Notes

Positioning: orderly handling for small teams with no legal ops. The product sells a calm registry and a dated trail inside clocks that counsel confirms.

Primary buyer: founder, support lead, or engineer at a SaaS team of 2-20 people.

Core promise (without outcome promises): every request moves through Received, Identity verified, Scoped, Fulfilled, Closed, with a 23-business-day internal SLA that sits inside the statutory clocks cited in the brief (30 days GDPR, 45 days several US states).

Differentiation:
- State machine with entry and exit criteria per stage, not a loose checklist.
- SLA-date formulas that surface due dates early.
- Source registry plus deletion runbook tracker for per-system evidence.
- Four letters with counsel-review points marked, including rejection with basis and extension notice.

Price $59: positioned as less than the cost of one rushed, ad-hoc request cycle (illustrative comparison). Value appears when one request stays organized and documented.

Use moments: first request arrives, verification uncertainty, multi-system search, deletion across backups, deadline pressure.

Non-goals: legal advice, automated deletion, third-party enforcement, promised outcomes.
