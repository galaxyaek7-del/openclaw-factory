# Value notes: Landlord Rent Ledger

Positioning: a calm record system for self-managing owners who want dated, traceable rent history without adopting collection software.

Primary value: daily entries that stay readable months later, with running balances computed by formula and every correction preserved as a new dated line.

Differentiation: one ledger per tenancy, oldest-balance-first application with lease-order respect, proof reference per line, fee lines tied to lease clauses with cap checks, and a notice-content checklist that pulls facts from the ledger before counsel prepares any notice.

Use moments: daily payment entry, weekly reconciliation, month-end close, notice-content preparation with a cutoff, and January handoff to a CPA with organized summaries.

Boundaries: preparation and organization only. Legal sufficiency and tax treatment are determined by counsel and CPA under verified local rules. No outcome promised.
