# Value notes: DTC Returns Abuse Playbook

Positioning: a fair manual system for small DTC teams that need to curb abusive returns without adding friction for sound customers.

Primary value: every return logged the same way with structured reasons, photo status, inspection facts, and a formula score that ranks review priority at the account level.

Differentiation: tiered paths that stay welcoming by default, graduated warn-before-restrict steps with written reasons and appeal, plus monthly tuning that adjusts weights and thresholds from logged outcomes.

Use moments: daily intake and inspection, review-before-decision on flagged cases, denial and appeal handling with letters, and a monthly tuning review.

Boundaries: triage and workflow only. Scores do not decide alone. Policy fit with consumer rules and processor terms requires separate review. No outcome promised.
