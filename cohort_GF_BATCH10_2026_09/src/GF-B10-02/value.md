Value and positioning - Sales Tax Nexus Tracker ($79)
Buyer: ecommerce founder or marketplace seller adding states faster than spreadsheets can track.
Shift: from year-end threshold surprises to a monthly loop where rolling totals, verification dates, and filing periods stay visible.
Why now: threshold structures shifted in 2024-2026 with several states dropping transaction tests, so remembered rules mislead.
Differentiation: channel-separated log plus state-source verification discipline plus registration-to-calendar close rule, packaged for CPA handoff rather than ending at a total.
Proof of work: four linked workbook sheets with rolling-sum and aging formulas; three templates that carry explicit questions to the CPA.
Honest boundary: no threshold stated as current fact, no filing decision made by any formula, CPA verification required. That boundary keeps the tool durable across amendments.
Use: monthly close and read, quarterly re-verification of high-sales states, weekly calendar review.
