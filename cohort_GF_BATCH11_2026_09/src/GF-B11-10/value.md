# Landlord Move-In Inspection Pack - Positioning Notes

## Slot
GF-B11-10 - slug landlord_movein_inspection_pack - price $49.
Audience: independent landlords documenting condition at move-in. Condition documentation only.

## Problem in plain terms
When move-out charges are disputed, the question is narrow: what did the unit look like on day one, and can both parties prove it. Without a signed baseline plus timestamped photos, deductions are hard to support. Several states mandate checklists or delivery timelines, which adds a procedural layer on top of the factual one.

## What this pack does
Provides a walkthrough-ready system: room-by-room notes linked to photo IDs, a per-photo log with coverage counts, a signature tracker with days-from-move-in math and missing-signature counts, plain wear-vs-damage wording reference, and state-notice rule notes. Includes a printable condition report form and tenant acknowledgment packet with space for tenant-added notes.

## What it does not do
Does not track rent, balances, or receipts. Explicitly distinct from rent-ledger products. Does not provide legal advice. Does not decide wear vs damage as a legal matter. Users must verify state rules locally.

## Differentiation
Ledger tools track money. This pack tracks facts about physical condition. The link between each written note and a photo ID, plus same-day signatures and copy delivery, is the core value. Coverage checks before leaving the property prevent thin files.

## Price logic
$49 is positioned against a single disputed cleaning or paint charge, which often exceeds that amount. The pitch is records that both parties signed and can read months later.

## Risks and honest limits
State rules vary on checklists, delivery method, and deposit deductions. Wear language is guidance for consistent notes, not a legal standard. Positioning must avoid outcome promises. Include the disclosure on every sales page: verify state rules locally; not legal advice.
