# Positioning Notes: Real Estate Transaction Deadline Kit

## Audience in one line
Solo agents and transaction coordinators carrying several residential files at once who feel every deadline personally and want one screen that shows all of them.

## Pain and cost of inaction
Fifty to seventy tasks per deal scatter across email, text, and memory. Contingency windows close without required notices, leverage is lost, extensions are negotiated from weakness, and in the worst cases files fall apart. Missing documents surface the day before closing. The cost is lost commissions, stressed clients, and strained co-op relationships.

## Distinct position
Agent-side operations, explicitly not buyer-side. The kit tracks, reminds, collects, and closes. It never advises buyers on offers, waivers, or negotiation, and it repeats the not-legal-advice boundary in the guide, the listing, and the message template. Verify-state-rules-locally is part of the product promise, not fine print.

## Why this format wins
One spreadsheet with cross-file urgency beats both memory and buried platform screens. Ten minutes per file per Monday, ending with sent messages. No new software to learn, no monthly fee, works alongside any broker platform as the urgency layer.

## Price logic
Priced at $59 against the value of a single saved file: one protected commission covers it many times over in typical solo-practice experience. Position as file insurance through routine, not as software.

## Proof strategy
No testimonials, no ratings, no statistics are claimed. Proof is structural: show the sorted contingency view, the missing-document flags, the Monday routine, and the post-close learning loop. Buyers evaluate the operating logic, not promises.

## Objection handling
Already have a broker platform: this is the cross-file urgency view those platforms bury, usable alongside them. Counting rules differ by state: the kit teaches local verification first and tracks entered dates faithfully. Too many files for spreadsheets: the review cadence and contingency discipline transfer directly into dedicated software when volume justifies it. Want legal answers: broker or counsel same day, stated plainly and repeatedly.

## Risks and honest limits
Requires a named owner running the Monday review without exception; skipped weeks recreate the original problem. Cannot verify counting methods or confirm delivery. Does not interpret contracts. Caps around ten to fifteen concurrent files before dedicated software fits better.
