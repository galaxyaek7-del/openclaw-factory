# Cottage Food Compliance Kit - Positioning Notes

## Slot
GF-B11-09 - slug cottage_food_compliance_kit - price $49.
Audience: US home-based food producers (cottage food operators) selling allowed foods direct to consumers.

## Problem in plain terms
Each state writes its own cottage food rules. Caps differ. Registration steps differ. Label wording differs. Reforms dated 2025 in TX, VT, IA, OK, NV, MN moved several thresholds and procedures. Producers also face allergen labeling on every product: nine major allergens plus a home-kitchen warning where required. A missed line on a label or an expired registration can stall a market day.

## What this kit does
Organizes four recurring jobs in one place: tracking state requirements, checking labels before print, logging registrations and renewals, and tracking sales against the cap with simple formulas. Includes a label template and stall signage text so daily use stays consistent.

## What it does not do
Does not provide legal advice. Does not file permits. Does not test food or approve labels. Does not cover wholesale or licensed-facility foods. Every sheet directs the user to verify state rules locally.

## Allergen module placement
Allergen review lives inside the Label Compliance Checker and the label template (Contains statement, shared-equipment note, home-kitchen warning). No separate allergen product is included or required.

## Differentiation
Many label books show example labels. This kit adds operational tracking: dated verification sources, expiration formulas, HOLD vs PASS logic, and cumulative cap math. The emphasis is on repeatable monthly routine rather than one-time reading.

## Price logic
$49 matches a single market-day supply run for many operators. The pitch is simple: one organized binder that supports calmer inspections, renewals, and reprints.

## Risks and honest limits
Rules change. Caps are illustrated with example figures only. Users must re-verify sources. Positioning must avoid outcome promises and legal-advice framing. Include the disclosure on every sales page: verify your state rules locally; not legal advice.
