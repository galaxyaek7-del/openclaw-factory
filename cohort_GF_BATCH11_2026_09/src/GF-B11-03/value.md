# Value Notes - Restaurant Food Cost Control Kit (GF-B11-03)

## Buyer and willingness to pay
Independent operators feel food cost weekly at the bank balance. Price $49 sits below one small produce order, so the buying question is whether the routine saves one bad order a month, not whether the files are long.

## Differentiation
Most food cost advice explains the ratio and stops. This kit ties five sheets into one weekly ritual: count, cost, compare, waste-log, reorder. The variance dashboard is the link competitors skip.

## Proof points used in listing
NRA industry figures frame the 28 to 35 percent band as background context. Everything else is labeled illustrative so the buyer trusts the math without suspecting invented claims.

## Objections handled
Time: first week is setup, then 60 to 90 minutes weekly. Small kitchen: start with fewer rows. Large menu: cost top 10 first. Software: standard spreadsheet formulas, no macros.

## Scope guardrails
Single unit only. No multi-unit consolidation, no liquor inventory, no live price feeds. Guardrails keep support load low and the product honest about what it does.

## Price justification
$49 against waste reduction framing: one caught over-portioning pattern or one corrected par level covers the price many times over in a normal week. No outcome promise is made; the mechanism is the selling point.
