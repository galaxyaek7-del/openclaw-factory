# Value Notes - Salon Commission Pay Kit (GF-B11-04)

## Buyer and willingness to pay
Salon owners dread payday arguments. Price $39 sits below one disputed payout correction, so the buying question is whether one clean payday is worth it.

## Differentiation
Generic commission advice gives percentage ranges and stops. This kit gives exact cell formulas, a per-stylist statement that shows its arithmetic, and a dispute log with a 48-hour rule. Verifiability is the feature.

## Proof points used in listing
No industry statistics are claimed. All numbers and names are labeled illustrative. Trust comes from provable arithmetic, not from cited averages.

## Objections handled
Setup: one pay period of data entry. Small team: works at two chairs. Missed tiers: routine still documents the period. Booth rental: explicitly excluded in who_not_for so the wrong buyer self-selects out.

## Scope guardrails
Commission payroll only. No payroll tax computation, no tip reporting, no booth rental accounting, one tier threshold per period. Guardrails prevent misuse and keep the agreement template reviewable by a local professional.

## Price justification
$39 against dispute cost framing: one avoided argument or one corrected void covers the price. No payout or approval promise is made; the written record is the selling point.
