## Difference in value

Every comparable found is a single artifact (one tracker, or one report form). Ours bundle a method guide, a calculating workbook and editable templates. That justifies a premium over the single-artifact ceiling. It does not justify 3x: at $59 the real-estate kit is about 3x the highest comparable seen ($20), and the landlord pack at $49 is about 2.6x ($19). There is no evidence of a buyer paying either price. A strong free substitute exists in both markets.

## Proposed prices (judgment, disclosed)

Rule: at most about 1.5x the highest comparable observed, reflecting the three-component bundle, rounded to a common price point.

| Kit | Now | Proposed | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real Estate Transaction Deadline Kit | $59 | **$29** | 1.45x the $20 ceiling |
| Landlord Move-In Inspection Pack | $49 | **$27** | 1.42x the $19 ceiling |

What would justify a higher price later: a real sale at the current price, a sold-count signal from a comparable at a higher price, or a demonstrably deeper deliverable (state-specific deadlines, for example). Absent those, holding the current prices is a guess.

## Offer improvements, not price-only

- Both listings have no cover image; real-page covers, as done for the two launch products, would help more than a price change alone.
- Lead each sales page with what competitors lack: the calculating workbook and the method guide, not just "a checklist".
