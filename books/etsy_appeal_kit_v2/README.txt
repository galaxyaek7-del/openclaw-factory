Etsy Suspension Appeal Kit v2 — file list
============================================
1. etsy_suspension_appeal_kit.pdf (19 pages) — the full method: triage, Appeals Center
   walkthrough, evidence gathering, letter framework, policy fixes, case management,
   prevention, filing checklist.
2. etsy_appeal_letter_template_A_IP.docx — fill-in reinstatement letter for IP/trademark cases.
3. etsy_appeal_letter_template_B_policy.docx — fill-in reinstatement letter for policy violations.
4. etsy_appeal_evidence_tracker.xlsx — evidence log, deadline tracker, open-case log.

How to use: read the PDF first, then pick the template matching your suspension type,
fill every [bracketed] field with your real facts, track everything in the spreadsheet.

Important: procedure information, not legal advice. An appeal never guarantees
reinstatement — Etsy decides. Templates contain placeholders; never submit false statements.
