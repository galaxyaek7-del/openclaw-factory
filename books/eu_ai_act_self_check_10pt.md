# EU AI Act 10-Point Self-Check (manual fulfillment asset, GF-41/C41)
# Sent by the founder by email when a lead submits the EU landing-page form.
# Regulatory info, not legal advice. Position as of October 2026 — verify against
# the official consolidated text (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 and Digital Omnibus amendments).

1. Have you listed every AI touchpoint your EU users meet (chatbots, assistants, recommenders, generated media)? (Y/N)
2. Does each conversational AI clearly disclose it is AI at first interaction? (Y/N)
3. Is AI-generated content (text/images/video shown to users) labeled as synthetic? (Y/N)
4. Have you classified each system: prohibited / high-risk / limited-risk / minimal-risk? (Y/N)
5. For any high-risk use: is there a named owner tracking the December 2, 2027 runway? (Y/N)
6. Do you use general-purpose AI models (own or third-party)? If yes, are provider duties mapped? (Y/N/N-A)
7. Is there a log of what changed, when, and who approved it (for future audit)? (Y/N)
8. Are vendor/third-party AI contracts checked for compliance responsibilities? (Y/N)
9. Is a serious-incident reporting path defined (who reports, to whom, how fast)? (Y/N)
10. Has someone with authority reviewed the answers above this quarter? (Y/N)

SCORING (honest, stated to buyer): 8-10 Yes = basic posture in place, keep monitoring;
5-7 = gaps worth closing this quarter; 0-4 = start with inventory + disclosure now.
This checklist orients; it does not certify compliance. For a full implementation
path see the EU AI Act Compliance Toolkit ($155) or qualified counsel.
