# Professional Legal Case Research & Citation Verification
## One-Page Product Concept (Validation Concept — NOT a Launched Product)

> **STATUS: VALIDATION CONCEPT ONLY.** This describes a product that does not yet exist.
> Nothing here is for sale, nothing is being marketed, and no customers exist today.
> Purpose: a manual LinkedIn validation test with independent lawyers.

---

### 1. Problem (FACT — from verified evidence)
Independent lawyers research case law for every matter they handle, and do it manually.
Verified anchors: CoCounsel solo pricing is $519.35–639.20/user/mo (Thomson Reuters public
configurator); a legal buyer reports "talking to an IP attorney can go for $200+ an hour"
(news.ycombinator.com/item?id=32244691). The accepted pain analysis (2026-07-31) found 4
real pain-language hits across 10 real GitHub issues; urgency 40/100. Manual research on a
single case question consumes hours at a $200+/hr opportunity cost.

### 2. Proposed Outcome
Paste a case question -> receive a **structured legal-research memo** — relevant precedents
with real, URL-verifiable citations, holdings, and counter-arguments — in minutes instead of
hours. The memo supports (never replaces) attorney judgment. It is a tool, not legal advice.

### 3. Scope of the Future MVP (NOT built — future)
- One practice area per license (e.g., family law or personal injury).
- Input: a case question (text).
- Output: a research memo (relevant precedents, real citations, holdings, counter-arguments).
- Built-in cite-check rules; every citation links to a public, verifiable source.
- Disclaimer: support tooling, not legal advice; attorney final review required.

### 4. Differentiator (FACT — verified prices vs this concept)
- **One-time $194** — no subscription, no seat minimum, no annual contract.
- CoCounsel (solo) is $6,232–7,670/yr; Harvey is $288,000/yr with a 20-seat minimum
  (enterprise only). **$194 is under one month of the cheapest solo alternative.**
- Manual: $200+/hr × a 2–3 hr research task = $400–600 per case, repeated every case —
  vs a one-time $194 license.

### 5. Price
**$194 one-time** per practice-area license.
