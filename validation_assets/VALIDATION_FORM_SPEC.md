# Validation Form Specification — Professional Legal Case Research ($194)

> **PURPOSE:** Manual, founder-run validation with independent lawyers (and, per the
> evidence-first directive, other professional B2B markets if stronger evidence appears).
> No automated data collection, no payment, no payment-info collection, no personal-data
> storage beyond founder notes. This is a validation concept, NOT a launched product.

---

## Signal Taxonomy (A–E — every response maps to one or more)

| Signal | Meaning | Evidence weight |
|---|---|---|
| **A. General interest** | Generic positive engagement (like, share, "interesting") | **NOT purchase evidence** — recorded, never counted as intent |
| **B. Real customer pain** | Qualified respondent describes the problem from their own practice | Commercial signal (requires qualification) |
| **C. Willingness to pay** | Qualified respondent accepts a $194 one-time purchase (framed within Q2) | Strong commercial signal |
| **D. Willingness to reserve/buy** | Qualified respondent agrees to reserve a place at $194 if sample meets requirements | Strongest intent signal (Q2) |
| **E. Request for a sample** | Qualified respondent asks to see the sample memo to judge quality | Qualifying action (Q3) |

---

## Form Questions (3)

### Q1 — Pain + Frequency (signals B)
**"Is manual case research a real pain in your practice? How often does it come up?"**
- **Type:** single choice + frequency follow-up
- **Options:**
  - a) Yes — regularly, and it takes hours per matter
  - b) Somewhat — occasional but time-consuming
  - c) No — I delegate or it's not a problem
- **Frequency follow-up (free text):** "Approximately how many matters per week/month?"
- **Pain-positive if:** a) or b). Frequency recorded verbatim when given.

### Q2 — Willingness to Reserve/Buy (signals C + D) — primary purchase-intent question
**"Would you be willing to reserve a place for a $194 professional research package if
the validation sample meets your requirements?"**
- **Type:** single choice (exact options per founder directive)
- **Options:**
  - a) Yes — I would consider reserving it.
  - b) Maybe — I need to see the sample first.
  - c) No.
- **Price-positive (C):** a) selected. **Reservation-intent (D):** a) selected.
  b) is conditional and recorded as such — NOT counted as reservation intent.

### Q3 — Request for a Sample (signal E)
**"Would you like a sample memo to judge the quality yourself?"**
- **Type:** single choice + free-text practice area
- **Options:**
  - a) Yes — [practice area]
  - b) No
- **Sample request if:** a) selected (a qualified prospect's real action).

## Respondent Qualification
A respondent is **qualified** if they are an **independent lawyer** (solo practitioner or
small firm) who does their own case research — confirmed by the founder in the manual
conversation. Non-lawyers and employees of research-tool vendors are **not qualified**.
Per the evidence-first directive, the same qualification rule extends to any professional
B2B respondent (Compliance/GRC, Contract Management, Tax, Audit, Risk) if repositioning
evidence emerges — the respondent must actually work in that role and own the problem.

## Evidence Classification (apply to every recorded signal)
- **FACT** — verified, independently checkable (e.g., price anchors, public sources).
- **OBSERVED RESULT** — what the founder actually observed in this sprint (real responses).
- **INFERENCE** — reasoned conclusion from observed results, labeled as inference.
- **RECOMMENDATION** — proposed next step, never presented as fact.
- **Source links** recorded whenever available (real URLs).

## Minimum Validation Targets (first sprint — per founder directive)
- **New genuine pain signals (B):** >= 3 qualified.
- **Qualified willingness-to-pay/reservation signals (C/D):** >= 25, OR a clearly strong
  smaller sample with high purchase intent (e.g., >= 50% of qualified respondents = D).
- **Qualified waitlist/interest signals:** >= 25 if traffic volume supports it.
- **Qualified conversion rate:** >= 25% is a strong positive signal, not an automatic requirement.
- **Independent evidence sources:** >= 3.
- **Real sample requests (E):** >= 1 from a qualified prospect.

## Rules
- Likes/generic comments (A) are never treated as purchase evidence.
- Competitor pricing is never treated as proof customers will pay $194.
- Numbers are never manipulated to reach targets.
