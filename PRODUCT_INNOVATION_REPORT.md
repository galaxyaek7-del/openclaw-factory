Concepts tested, MVPs built, paid pilots, average validation cost, and average time-to-validation are all honestly `UNKNOWN`/0 — no real tracking exists for any of them. **Revenue generated by innovation: $0.** Never optimized for idea count alone — the directive's own explicit rule.
