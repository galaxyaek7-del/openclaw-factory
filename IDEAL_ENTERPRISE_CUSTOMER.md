# Galaxy Forge — Ideal Enterprise Customer Profile

**Date:** 2026-08-08 | ADR-220, Phase 30, Section 5. `enterprise_sales_engine.ideal_enterprise_customer_profile()`.

---

## The 13 required fields

Industry, Company Size, Revenue, Operational Complexity, Problem Severity, Digital Maturity, Budget, Decision Structure, Technology Environment, Growth, Regulatory Complexity, Potential Contract Value, Recurring Potential.

## A schema, not a fabricated example

The directive asked for an ICP prioritizing "customers with expensive recurring problems" — this is a real, ready schema, never populated with an invented example company ahead of real evidence. Filling it requires a real discovered account (see `ENTERPRISE_OPPORTUNITY_REGISTRY.md`'s honestly-empty Account Registry).

## Real, honest state

**0 real enterprise accounts exist to derive an ICP from.** The one real signal this factory has close to enterprise buying behavior is the EU AI Act Compliance Toolkit's real target buyer (accounting firm compliance officers) — a premium/prosumer product, not an enterprise deal, and not force-mapped onto this schema.
