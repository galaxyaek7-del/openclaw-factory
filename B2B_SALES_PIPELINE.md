## No fabricated pipeline depth

The directive's own Section 19 names 11 stages precisely so a real pipeline can show real depth over time — this document deliberately does not pre-populate stages 2-11 with plausible-sounding entries. A pipeline with 1 honest `TARGET` entry is a more useful signal than one with 10 fabricated ones.
