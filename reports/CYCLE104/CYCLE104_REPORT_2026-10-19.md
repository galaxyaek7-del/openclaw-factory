## 9. Lessons worth keeping (factory knowledge)

1. **A live listing is not evidence that the right file is live.** Three products
   matched their canonical artifacts byte-for-byte; one did not. Byte comparison
   against the local canonical artifact is now the reusable check
   (`scripts/verify_gumroad_listing.py`), and it must be re-run whenever a product
   is re-uploaded.
2. **A field that the platform does not expose must be reported as absent, not
   guessed.** Gumroad returns `name: null` on attachments; matching moved to
   `(filetype, size)` and the residual uncertainty is stated on every row.
3. **A harness limitation must never read as a product verdict.** `MISSING_DATA`
   on `market_reality` is a property of the sweep, not of the ten products.
4. **A registry that is not derived from evidence will drift, and will overclaim in
   the founder's favor.** Four products were declared local-ready with no gate and
   failing content quality. Correction is now scripted and auditable.
5. **A module that declares itself the source of truth must contain every product
   family** — or it silently makes dashboards and revenue-per-product math wrong.
