| README | 11 × 1 | 0 | 0 |

Design decisions that exist for a reason:

- **Deadline state is a sentence, not a colour.** `OVERDUE n DAYS` / `DUE IN n DAYS` / `OPEN` / `CLOSED` / `NO DEADLINE SET` can be counted, sorted and read by someone who cannot distinguish colours.
- **Closed means closed.** Awarded, Declined and Withdrawn stop the deadline maths and are excluded from the "next deadline" helper. A four-year-old declined application does not keep shouting.
- **Duplicate detection is real, not cosmetic.** Two rows with the same funder *and* program are both flagged. Five years of grant registers fail this way.
- **Inputs and formulas are visually separated**: white cells are the user's, blue cells are computed, yellow rows are examples.
- **Examples use real Excel dates**, not text. This is a deliberate improvement over B12-005, where text dates left every computed column blank in the shipped example.
- **A row check names concrete problems** in plain language instead of hiding them: missing funder, max below min, Submitted with no date, Awarded with no amount, missing deadline, awarded above the recorded range.

Independent re-derivation (Python, not Excel) on the four example rows:

| Example | Deadline | Status | Days | State | Duplicate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EX-001 | 2027-03-15 | Drafting | 147 | OPEN | flagged |
| EX-002 | 2026-11-02 | Submitted | 14 | DUE IN 14 DAYS | — |
| EX-003 | 2026-07-01 | Awarded | -110 | CLOSED | — |
| EX-004 | 2027-03-15 | Drafting | 147 | OPEN | flagged (deliberate duplicate of EX-001) |

Timeline re-derivation: annual report first due 2027-01-31 → OPEN; quarterly first due 2026-12-15 → next 2026-12-15 → OPEN; a row with no first due date → `Check first due and repeat days` / `CHECK INPUTS`.

**Economics disclosure:** the auto-distribution path would silently re-price a 17-page `gumroad_digital` product to $15.00. The founder-authorized first-sale path publishes a manifest price directly, which is why the cap is enforced here deliberately rather than bypassed.
