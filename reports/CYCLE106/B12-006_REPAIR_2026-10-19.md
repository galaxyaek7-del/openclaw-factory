**Outcome:** `QA_PASS` on both scored layers. Publication not attempted.

## What was actually wrong

The Cycle-104 report said "too thin". That was true and it was the smaller problem.

Reading the pre-repair builder (`git show HEAD:scripts/build_batch12_xlsx.py`) showed the tool
itself was a lie in the one place a buyer would look first:

| Cell | What it said | What it was |
|---|---|---|
| `SpaceMeasure!B3` | `Office % (= office ÷ total)` | the number `0.125` |
| `SpaceMeasure!C3` | `=B3/B2 — used for Home % above` | a note about a formula that was not there |
| `Expenses2026!E2` (example row) | a share of the home | a second hard-coded number, `0.40`, disagreeing with `0.125` |

Changing the square footage would not have changed the deduction. Every later row was blank in
the `Home %` column, so the "Deductible estimate" was whatever number the user typed multiplied by
the amount. There was no method comparison, no annual view, no validation, and the percent number
format was `"#,##0.00%` — unterminated, so even a correct value would have displayed wrong.

None of this is visible to a value-only check. The file was self-consistent.

## What was rebuilt

Two artifacts, both rebuilt rather than patched.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Guide PDF | 3,270 B · 4 sections · 254 words | **18,614 B · 7 pages · 3,352 words** · 14 sections |
| Workbook | 7,886 B · 4 sheets · 3 formulas | **16,462 B · 7 sheets · 111 formulas · 3 validations** |

Workbook sheets: `Expenses2026`, `SpaceMeasure`, `MethodCompare`, `AnnualSummary`, `Categories`,
`Checklist`, `README`.

The calculation is now one that runs:

- `SpaceMeasure!B4` = `IF(AND(ISNUMBER(B2),ISNUMBER(B3),B2>0,B3>0,B3<=B2),B3/B2,"Check B2 and B3…")`
- `SpaceMeasure!B8` = `MIN(B3,B7)*B6` — the simplified method with the 300 sq ft cap
- `Expenses2026!E` reads `SpaceMeasure!$B$4` on all 40 rows; `F` computes `D*E`
- `MethodCompare` puts both methods on the same eligible costs and writes a verdict sentence
- `AnnualSummary` sums each category with `SUMIFS` and states the three limits as rows

The guide carries the arithmetic the workbook performs: 150 sq ft of 1,200 = 12.5%; simplified
= $750; actual = $22,200 × 12.5% = $2,775; the difference, the $1,500 cap on 400 sq ft, the
gross-income limit and the depreciation tradeoff on sale. Two defects were found in the guide
during the rebuild and fixed: the block had lost its list structure (a stray bracket, now
repaired and the file parses clean) and the arithmetic was stated but not shown.

## Price — disclosed, not changed

The manifest price is $29 and **has not been changed**. The honest reading from
`economics.market_realism_check`:

| Tier | $29 | Maximum accepted |
|---|---|---|
| `gumroad_digital` | rejected | **$7.00** (the $1/page ceiling binds at 7 pages) |
| `gumroad_elite` | accepted | $29.00 |

The auto-distribution path uses the general tier, so left alone it would silently re-price this
product from $29 to $7.00. The policy counts PDF pages only; the 7-sheet, 111-formula workbook —
the part a buyer actually uses — is invisible to it. Same shape already recorded for B12-010.

The guide was extended to 3,352 words because the product needed that content, **not** to raise the
page count toward a ceiling. No filler was added.

This is a founder decision, so it is disclosed with both readings and left open.
