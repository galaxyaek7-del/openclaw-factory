## Why the ranking is what it is

1. **systeme.io direct** is the only row with zero cost, no application, no
   deposit, and a payout rail (PayPal) the founder already has. The affiliate
   terms read in April 2026 state 60% lifetime recurring, cookie lifetime
   attribution, $30 minimum, paid monthly. Those terms make a single referral
   worth $10.20/month on the $17 plan and $58.20/month on $97.
2. **impact.com** is free and explicitly lists Algeria in both country fields,
   but approval is discretionary and a $10/month dormancy fee applies after six
   unpaid months. That fee is a real cost if the account is opened and left
   idle, so it is not "usable now" without a plan.
3. **Digistore24** retains 10% for 60 days and its minimum payout is 250 units.
   Both are workable at volume, neither is workable at zero.
4. **PartnerStack** has the lowest minimum ($5) but the payout routes are not
   verified for Algeria.
5. **Awin** is excluded because a refundable deposit is still spend.
6. **Amazon Associates** is excluded for two independent reasons: no tag is
   configured, and the public site is not proven to serve the click route.

## The payout constraint that outranks commission rate

PayPal's Algeria receiving terms (updated 22 September 2026) require a US bank
account or a Visa card, impose a 30-day hold, and charge $5 for a manual
transfer to a Visa. A 60% lifetime commission on a $17 plan is $10.20; one
withdrawal can consume half of a month's commission. This is a structural
constraint, not a reason to pick a different network: the same money has to
arrive somehow.

## Facts this matrix does not establish

- Whether any Algerian bank accepts a wire from systeme.io, or what it charges.
- Whether a payout can be received at all without a tax residency certificate.
- Whether impact.com would approve this site (no application submitted).
- Whether the 60% rate applies to the affiliate's own referred customers only,
  or to their sub-affiliates as well (not stated on the page read).
- Refund and clawback behaviour for systeme.io commissions (not stated).
