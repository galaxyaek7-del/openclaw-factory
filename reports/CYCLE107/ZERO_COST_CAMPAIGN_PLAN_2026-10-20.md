# Zero-Cost Campaign Plan

Date: 2026-10-20
Budget: $0. No ads, no paid tools, no purchased traffic, no lead lists.

## The asset

"systeme.io free plan: what it covers, and the three limits that force an
upgrade" (1,032 words). It is written as a decision aid, not a pitch: it names
the cases where the reader should not upgrade. That is deliberate. An affiliate
article that recommends the free plan when the free plan is correct earns trust
once; one that pushes an upgrade earns a click once.

## Distribution options that cost nothing

| Option | Cost | Control | Risk | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Publish on the factory's public customer site | $0 | Full | Site currently has no verified public traffic | HOLD_UNTIL_HOST_VERIFIED |
| Founder's own audience (newsletter, profile, community) | $0 | Full | Audience size is UNKNOWN to this session | FOUNDER_ONLY |
| Answer an existing question where the limits are already being discussed | $0 | Full | Most communities restrict self-promotion; rules must be read first | HOLD_PENDING_POLICY_CHECK |
| Republish on a free writing platform | $0 | Partial | Platform may strip affiliate links or require disclosure format | UNKNOWN |

Nothing in this table is a traffic estimate. The plan does not assume readers
exist; it tests whether they do.

## The experiment

- **Duration:** 14 days from first publication.
- **Cost:** $0.
- **Single asset, single link.** No A/B test until there is traffic to split.
- **Measure:** page views, outbound clicks, click referrers, and any real
  partner-dashboard transaction. Clicks are counted locally; transactions are
  read from the partner, never inferred.
- **Do not measure:** impressions the platform does not report, "engagement",
  or estimated conversion rate. None of these are observable with the current
  instrumentation.

## Decision rule, written before the data

| Observation after 14 days | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 page views | The distribution channel is wrong. Do not rewrite the article. Change the placement. |
| Views but 0 clicks | The placement or the CTA is wrong. The article's honesty is not the problem; check that the link is visible above the fold and in the CTA line. |
| Clicks but 0 transactions | Expected at low volume. Keep the asset, do not add pressure tactics, and do not claim the channel works. |
| A real transaction appears | Record it from the partner dashboard with its transaction id. That is the first real affiliate commission, and the first evidence about conversion, not proof of a repeatable channel. |
| Any commission reversed | Record the reversal as it appears in the partner dashboard. Do not silently net it. |

No number in this table is a target. A 0-click result is a result.

## Policy checklist before publishing anywhere

1. The disclosure is visible before the link, not only in a footer.
2. The destination platform permits affiliate links in this format.
3. The article does not claim a discount, bonus, or urgency that does not exist.
4. Price and plan limits match the page read on 2026-10-20, or are updated.
5. No client-side tracking beyond the local click record and the network's own
   cookie. No fingerprinting, no cross-site profile.

## What would make this plan wrong

If the target audience is not people deciding on a funnel tool, no amount of
placement fixes it. The honest test is one real reader arriving from a real
source, then one click. Until that happens, the plan is a set of stop rules,
not a forecast.
