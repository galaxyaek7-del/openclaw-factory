## 4. EU AI Act Toolkit live-vs-local mismatch — false alarm, real trap found

The Cycle-104 `PUBLICATION_MISMATCH` was **a false alarm caused by a stale canonical
filename** (**OBSERVED**):

| Revision | Bytes | sha256 (prefix) | Text of the claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| `books/eu_ai_act_compliance_toolkit.pdf` | 139,121 | `c22671a674141e…` | "**ensure** compliance" ×2 — withdrawn guarantee |
| `books/eu_ai_act_compliance_toolkit_v2.pdf` | 139,401 | `18c4158342c7f8a2…` | "**pursue** compliance" ×2 — repaired (Cycle 86) |

The live listing serves the **139,401-byte repaired v2**, so the product live today is the
correct one. The genuine defect found underneath: **`data/asset_registry.json` GF-001
pointed at the defective v1**, so any re-upload driven by that registry would have
silently republished the withdrawn claim.

Fixed: GF-001 now points at `books/eu_ai_act_compliance_toolkit_v2.pdf`, with both
revisions' sizes, hashes and provenance recorded in `known_defects`. The superseded
revision is **kept on disk as an audit trail, not deleted**. Secondary, unreconciled:
the local preview is 106,489 B against a recorded live preview of 106,314 B (175 bytes)
— disclosed, not explained.
