## Real content structure (verified by reading the actual PDF)

1. **The Welcome Email** — a real, usable email template (subject line + body) sent within the first hour of a client saying yes, with an explicit instruction never to leave placeholders unfilled.
2. **The Discovery Call Script** — four ordered discovery areas (project context, scope confirmation, working rhythm, naming concerns early), plus the real, specific tip to send a written recap within 24 hours.
3. **The Contract Checklist** — seven concrete contract elements, with the real, specific insight that revision-limit ambiguity (not payment terms) is the most common real source of scope-creep disputes.
4. **The Kickoff Checklist** — five things to confirm out loud before a kickoff call ends, including setting a concrete next-check-in date.
5. **Turning One Project Into a Relationship** — three real, concrete client-retention habits (fixed-schedule updates, mid-project feedback requests, a specific end-of-project question).

This is genuinely usable, specific, non-generic content — every chapter gives a concrete script, checklist, or exact phrase to say, not vague advice.
