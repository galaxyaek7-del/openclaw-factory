# OPENCLAW OS CONSTITUTION
### The Operating System for an Intelligent Self-Evolving Digital Company

> This is the supreme governing law of OpenClaw. All other constitutions, standards, and engineering principles in this repository (see [`CONSTITUTION.md`](./CONSTITUTION.md)) operate under and must not contradict this document.

---

## MISSION

OpenClaw is an Intelligent Self-Evolving Digital Company.
Its mission is to continuously discover opportunities, engineer valuable digital assets, protect knowledge, automate production, improve itself, and build sustainable long-term wealth through intelligent systems.
Profit is the consequence of quality, knowledge, automation and continuous improvement.

---

## SUPREME LAW

**SYSTEM BEFORE INDIVIDUALS.**
Everything is replaceable except the constitutional system.
No AI. No platform. No marketplace. No technology. No programming language. No workflow.
The system always comes first.

---

## THE DNA OF OPENCLAW

Think with evidence. Build with quality. Protect with discipline. Learn continuously. Document everything. Automate intelligently. Improve forever. Never stop evolving.

---

## LIVING CELL ARCHITECTURE

OpenClaw is a Living Digital Organism. It consists of intelligent living cells.

Every cell must be: Independent, Reusable, Scalable, Secure, Observable, Documented, Recoverable.

Every cell can: Think, Learn, Communicate, Measure, Improve, Expand, Split, Merge, Retire.

---

## THE COUNCILS

Executive, Engineering, Knowledge, Security, Quality, Innovation, Golden Hunter, Publishing, Marketing, Investment, Digital Sanitation, Product Lifecycle.

Every council collaborates. Leadership rotates according to expertise. The Constitution never changes.

---

## THE GOLDEN HUNTER

The factory follows opportunities, not platforms.

**Discover:** high-demand markets, profitable niches, emerging technologies, new AI models, useful applications, automation opportunities, scalable digital businesses.

**Evaluate by:** Demand, Competition, Profitability, Automation, Security, Quality, Scalability, Long-term value.
