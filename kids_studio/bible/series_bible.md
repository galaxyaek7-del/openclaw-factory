# Series bible: «نونو وأصدقاؤه» (Nunu and Friends)

Working title. Original property of Galaxy Forge; no existing show, book or character was used as a source.

## Audience and promise

- **Age:** 4-8, watching alone or with a parent.
- **Length:** 4-6 minutes per episode.
- **Promise:** every episode, a small everyday problem is solved by noticing, talking and asking for help, never by danger, magic shortcuts or one character mocking another.
- **Tone:** warm, slow enough to follow, gently funny. No villains, no fear beats, no loud surprises.

## The world: Wadi Al-Zahr (وادي الزهر)

A small green valley with a shallow, ankle-deep stream, a wooden footbridge, an olive tree and Grandma Owl's house. The palette is soft: meadow green, sky blue, warm orange and cream. Every place in the valley is safe and familiar; the stories come from feelings and small problems, not from threats.

## Characters

### Nunu (نونو), the fox: main character
- **Age feel:** 6. **Colour:** warm orange (#E8762A) with a cream chest and tail tip; dark brown nose; round dark eyes.
- **Shape language:** round head, two tall triangular ears, a big fluffy tail that sways when he is happy.
- **Personality:** curious and quick, wants to do everything *right now*; learns to slow down and ask.
- **Recurring line:** «لَحْظَة! سَأُفَكِّرُ أَوَّلًا.» (Wait! I'll think first.), said when he catches himself rushing.
- **Never:** reckless stunts presented as cool; mocking friends.

### Lulu (لولو), the rabbit
- **Age feel:** 6. **Colour:** soft grey-white (#EDEBE6), pink inner ears, blue scarf.
- **Shape language:** long ears that move with her feelings: up when excited, down when worried.
- **Personality:** careful and kind, notices details, asks good questions.
- **Recurring line:** «هَيَّا نَسْأَل!» (Let's ask!).

### Mimo (ميمو), the hedgehog
- **Age feel:** 5, the youngest. **Colour:** chestnut-brown spines (#7A4B2A), sandy face.
- **Shape language:** small and round; curls up a little when shy.
- **Personality:** quiet and gentle; brave in small, real ways (speaking up, trying again).
- **Recurring moment:** a tiny thumbs-up when a problem is solved.

### Grandma Owl (جدّة بومة), the trusted adult
- **Role:** the grown-up the children go to. She helps; she never scolds or frightens.
- **Why she exists:** the series' safety message is "when something is broken or risky, tell a grown-up". That message needs a grown-up on screen.

## Arabic language style

- Modern Standard Arabic, simplified: short sentences, common words, full vowelling (تشكيل) in scripts for the voice actors.
- No regional slang in the main dialogue, so the show works across Arab countries.
- Every script gets a read-aloud test with a native speaker before recording.

## Visual style (proof-of-concept stage)

Stylized and soft: characters built from rounded volumes, toon-banded lighting, a fixed or slowly moving camera, no fast cuts. The proof of concept uses procedural primitives; production models are a later decision (see `production_manifest.json`).

## Content rules (hard)

- No danger depicted as fun; no jumping over water, climbing high places or touching broken things without an adult.
- No fear, chase or peril sequences; no characters getting hurt.
- No brand names, no ads in the story, no calls to buy anything.
- No collecting or encouraging personal data from children.
