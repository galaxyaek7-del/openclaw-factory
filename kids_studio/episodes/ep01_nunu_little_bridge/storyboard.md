## Shot 01 detail (proof of concept)

- **Set:** two grass banks with a shallow stream between them; a wooden footbridge (deck plus two rails) across the stream; distant hills; soft sky.
- **Nunu:** starts off-screen left at x = -4.6; walks at a steady pace (no running) to x = -2.2, on the grass in front of the bridge; stops; tail sways twice.
- **Lulu:** far bank, x = +2.3, ears up; a small ear wiggle when Nunu arrives.
- **Mimo:** beside Lulu, x = +2.9; a small happy bounce at the end.
- **Camera:** fixed for the whole shot.
- **No dialogue** in the proof of concept: no Arabic voice is available yet (see `rights_ledger.json`).
