# الحلقة ١: «نونو والجسر الصغير»
# Episode 1: "Nunu and the Little Bridge"

- **Status:** DRAFT v0.1. Not approved. Needs (1) native Arabic review of wording and vowelling (تشكيل), (2) read-aloud test, (3) child-safety sign-off in `qa_checklist.md`.
- **Target length:** about 4.5 minutes.
- **Lesson:** when something is broken or wobbly, we don't take risks; we tell a grown-up.
- **Characters:** Nunu, Lulu, Mimo, Grandma Owl.

> Note to the reviewer: the series bible's recurring lines are rendered here in Modern Standard Arabic, «لَحْظَة! سَأُفَكِّرُ أَوَّلًا.» and «هَيَّا نَسْأَل!», so that all dialogue stays in one register.

---

### المشهد ١: صباح في وادي الزهر (Scene 1: morning in the valley), about 20 s
*A sunny meadow. A small stream, ankle-deep, with a wooden footbridge. Lulu and Mimo are on the far bank. Nunu walks happily from the left toward the bridge.*

**نونو** (بفرح): صَبَاحُ الخَيْرِ يَا وَادِي الزَّهْر! لُولُو وَمِيمُو يَنْتَظِرَانِنِي!
*(Good morning, Flower Valley! Lulu and Mimo are waiting for me!)*

### المشهد ٢: اللوح المتحرّك (Scene 2: the wobbly plank), about 40 s
*Nunu steps onto the first plank and stops. The middle plank tilts slightly. Gentle "creak" sound, not scary.*

**نونو**: أُوه! هَذَا اللَّوْحُ يَهْتَزّ!
*(Oh! This plank is wobbling!)*

**نونو** (يتراجع خطوة إلى الضفّة، steps back onto the bank): أَسْتَطِيعُ أَنْ أَقْفِزَ بِسُرْعَة... لَحْظَة! سَأُفَكِّرُ أَوَّلًا.
*(I could jump quickly... Wait! I'll think first.)*

> Staging note: Nunu never jumps and never mimes a jump. He steps back to safe ground before he speaks.

### المشهد ٣: الأصدقاء يساعدون (Scene 3: friends help), about 40 s
**لولو** (من الضفّة الأخرى، from the other bank): نُونُو! مَا الأَمْر؟
*(Nunu! What's the matter?)*

**نونو**: فِي الجِسْرِ لَوْحٌ مُخَلْخَل!
*(There's a loose plank in the bridge!)*

**لولو**: لَا تَقْفِزْ يَا نُونُو! هَيَّا نَسْأَل!
*(Don't jump, Nunu! Let's ask!)*

**ميمو** (بخجل، shyly): جَدَّةُ بُومَة... بَيْتُهَا قَرِيبٌ مِنْكَ، عِنْدَ شَجَرَةِ الزَّيْتُون.
*(Grandma Owl... her house is near you, by the olive tree.)*

### المشهد ٤: عند جدّة بومة (Scene 4: at Grandma Owl's), about 50 s
*Nunu walks (doesn't run) to the olive tree. Grandma Owl is on a low branch with a basket.*

**نونو**: جَدَّتِي بُومَة! فِي الجِسْرِ لَوْحٌ يَهْتَزّ.
*(Grandma Owl! A plank in the bridge is wobbling.)*

**جدّة بومة** (بلطف، warmly): أَحْسَنْتَ يَا نُونُو، لِأَنَّكَ لَمْ تَقْفِزْ وَجِئْتَ تُخْبِرُنِي. هَيَّا نُصْلِحُهُ مَعًا.
*(Well done, Nunu, for not jumping and coming to tell me. Let's fix it together.)*

### المشهد ٥: إصلاح الجسر (Scene 5: fixing the bridge), about 50 s
*Grandma Owl ties the plank firmly with rope. Nunu helps by carrying the soft rope basket. No tools are handed to the children.*

**جدّة بومة**: الحَبْلُ هُنَا... وَهُنَا. الآنَ صَارَ الجِسْرُ قَوِيًّا.
*(The rope goes here... and here. Now the bridge is strong.)*

**ميمو** (يرفع إبهامه، thumbs up): قَوِيٌّ جِدًّا!
*(Very strong!)*

### المشهد ٦: العبور معًا (Scene 6: crossing together), about 40 s
*Nunu crosses slowly, one paw on the rail. Lulu and Mimo cheer softly.*

**لولو**: أَحْسَنْتَ يَا نُونُو!
*(Well done, Nunu!)*

**نونو**: عِنْدَمَا نَرَى شَيْئًا مَكْسُورًا... نُخْبِرُ الكِبَار!
*(When we see something broken... we tell a grown-up!)*

**الجميع** (All): هَيَّا نَلْعَب!
*(Let's play!)*

### المشهد ٧: النهاية (Scene 7: ending), about 20 s
*Wide shot of the valley; the three friends play on the grass. Gentle closing music.*
