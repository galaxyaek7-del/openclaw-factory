Galaxy Forge — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 7, 2026

What Data We Collect

When you purchase a product:

  • Name (as provided at checkout)
  • Email address (for order confirmation and delivery)
  • Payment information (processed by Paddle/Gumroad — we do not store it)
  • Order details (product purchased, price, date)

When you visit our site:

  • Basic analytics (page views, referrer) — if analytics are enabled
  • No cookies beyond what's required for site functionality

We do NOT collect:

  • Social security numbers
  • Credit card numbers (processed by payment platforms)
  • Browsing history on other sites
  • Location data beyond country-level

Data Sharing

We share data with:
