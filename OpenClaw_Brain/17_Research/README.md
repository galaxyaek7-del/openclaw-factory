# 17 — Research

General-purpose folder for research notes that don't yet belong to a specific Council or Cell — market research, competitor analysis, technology evaluation, or anything explored before it has a home elsewhere in the Brain.

## Current state: empty

Nothing has been placed here yet. This is expected — most research to date (niche competition checks via `niche_validator_v2.py`, prompt-format investigation for `parseScoutBrief()`) happened inline during a specific build task and is documented at its point of use ([08_Market_Intelligence](../08_Market_Intelligence/), [09_Prompt_Library](../09_Prompt_Library/)) rather than as standalone research.

## When to use this folder

Add a dated `.md` file here when research doesn't immediately lead to a shipped component — e.g. "evaluated 3 alternative AI providers, chose to stay on Groq for now, here's why" — so the reasoning survives even if the immediate task doesn't touch code.
