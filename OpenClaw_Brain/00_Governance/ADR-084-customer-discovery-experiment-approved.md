# ADR-084 — Customer Discovery Experiment Approved; Execution Boundary Documented

**Date:** 2026-07-22
**Status:** Adopted. Founder approved running the 7-day real-conversation experiment for the Security Decision Copilot opportunity (Validation Score 78/100). **No PRD is written yet** — rescore after real conversations happen, per the founder's explicit instruction.

---

## The decision

Following the Customer Discovery Plan (published artifact, §9), the founder approved running the real-world validation experiment: build a target list, conduct 8–12 real discovery calls with actual SMB owners/execs, test pricing hypotheses (§12) and the human-backed-vs-pure-AI trust question directly, and rescore against the same 85-point threshold once real evidence exists — not before.

This closes the loop the prior Discovery Plan and Kill-Test Verdict both flagged as unresolved: every finding to date is desk research (cited market data, competitor analysis, adversarial diligence) — zero real conversations with an actual target-profile human have happened. That gap is exactly what this experiment exists to close.

## What this factory (Claude Code) can and cannot execute

Recorded plainly so a future session doesn't assume more automation exists here than actually does, and so the founder isn't surprised by what "approve the experiment" does and doesn't produce automatically:

**Can do (real, already available):**
- Real desk research — target-list building against the firmographic profile in the Discovery Plan §1 (company size, industry, real trigger signals like SOC2/vCISO-related public postings).
- Drafting outreach message templates, a structured call script, and a response-tracking sheet — grounded in the Discovery Plan's §2–§7 evidence, not invented.
- Synthesizing and rescoring real results once they exist.

**Cannot do (a real capability limit, not a policy choice):**
- Cannot conduct the actual discovery calls — a phone or video conversation with a real business owner requires a human on both ends. This factory has no calling/meeting capability.
- Will not simulate or fabricate interview responses to make the week appear complete. This is the same "never fabricate data" discipline that has governed every round of this investigation — it applies most strongly here, where the entire point of the experiment is to replace assumption with real evidence. A fabricated transcript would be worse than no experiment at all.

**Practical consequence:** the founder (or whoever is deputized to run outreach) needs to actually have the 8–12 conversations described in the Discovery Plan §9. This factory's role is preparation and synthesis on both sides of that gap, not the conversations themselves.

## Next action, if wanted

The Discovery Plan artifact (already published, favicon 🏛️) contains the full target profile, pain list, objection list, channel evidence, and pricing hypotheses needed to start outreach immediately. A condensed one-page call script / response-tracking sheet was not separately produced in this round — available as a fast follow if the founder wants it before starting calls, rather than assumed unneeded.

## Impact

- No code changed for this decision — process/governance only.
- Rescoring against the same 85-point threshold happens once real call outcomes exist, per the founder's explicit "do NOT write a PRD yet" instruction.
- If the rescore clears 85, the next artifact is the V1 PRD (per the original Customer Discovery directive's branching rule). If it doesn't, the opportunity is discarded honestly and hunting resumes — not before real evidence exists either way.
