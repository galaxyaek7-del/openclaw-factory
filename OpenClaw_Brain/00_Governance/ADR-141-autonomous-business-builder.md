# ADR-141 — Autonomous Business Builder (ABB)

**Date:** 2026-07-29
**Status:** Adopted.

---

## The directive

"EXECUTIVE DIRECTIVE — AUTONOMOUS BUSINESS BUILDER": for every Founder-approved opportunity, automatically create a "Business Blueprint" with 12 named sections (business model, revenue model, customer profile, competitor map, product roadmap, pricing strategy, marketing strategy, distribution strategy, launch checklist, risk assessment, growth plan, automation plan), 8 named estimates (development effort, expected monthly/yearly revenue, ROI, break-even time, recurring revenue potential, market durability, AI automation percentage), and a phased execution roadmap (each phase: objectives, tasks, dependencies, required AI agents, duration, success metrics, rollback plan). Mission Control exposes: Business Pipeline, Blueprint Status, Execution Progress, Investment Required, Expected Return, Priority, Risk, Confidence. Founder always approves before execution; no autonomous publishing, payments, or legal commitments.

## The one finding that reframed this build

A research audit (this session's own, file:line verified) found **near-total overlap with two already-real, already-wired modules**: `business_dossier.py::build_business_dossier()` (8 real sections, deterministic template, zero LLM narrative — built 2026-07-22, before this session's own work began — already called inside `opportunity_pipeline.annotate_decision()` for every real opportunity, and already rendered live in `mission_control.html`'s `renderBusinessDossier()`) and `production_blueprint.py::build_production_blueprint()` (a real 15-component blueprint reusing `business_dossier` + `value_engine` + `growth_engine` + `ai_capability.orchestrator` verbatim — its own docstring literally states "near-zero new computation"). `production_blueprint.py::build_production_missions_board()` already buckets every real ACCEPTED opportunity into 6 named production statuses (READY TO BUILD/BUILDING/QUALITY REVIEW/READY TO SELL/LIVE/LEARNING) — this already is the real substrate for ABB's "Business Pipeline"/"Blueprint Status" Mission Control ask, not something to rebuild.

10 of ABB's 12 named sections directly duplicate fields already in `business_dossier`/`production_blueprint` (Business model→`unique_value_proposition`, Revenue model→`revenue_projection`, Customer profile→`customer_persona`, Product roadmap→`product_specification`+`product_architecture`, Pricing strategy→`pricing_strategy`, Marketing strategy→`marketing_assets` (disclosed thin, ADR-108), Distribution strategy→`distribution_channels`, Launch checklist→`production_checklist`, Growth plan→`expansion_strategy`, Automation plan→`required_ai_models`). **2 are genuinely new**: Competitor map (real `competition`/`competitive_moat` fields exist but aren't assembled into one distinct section) and Risk assessment (`value_engine.py`'s real `at_risk` flag exists but isn't currently surfaced in either blueprint).

4 of ABB's 8 named estimates are direct citations of already-real fields built earlier this session (`capital_allocation_engine.py::investment_score()`'s `engineering_cost`→Development effort, `recurring_revenue_potential`→Recurring revenue potential, `automation_potential`→AI automation percentage; `value_engine`'s `board_summary.expected_roi`→ROI). **4 have zero real signal anywhere in this factory, confirmed by repo-wide grep before writing any code**: Expected monthly revenue, Expected yearly revenue, Break-even time, Market durability — `growth_engine.growth_forecast()` itself has no real forecasting algorithm yet (already disclosed `NOT ENOUGH EVIDENCE` by `strategic_intelligence_core.py`, 2 rounds earlier this session). These stay honestly `Unknown`, never invented from a near-zero real sales history.

**Decision: build a thin `autonomous_business_builder.py` that assembles ABB's exact 12-section/8-estimate/phased-roadmap shape almost entirely by citing `business_dossier`/`production_blueprint`/`investment_score`/`value_engine` verbatim, adding exactly 3 genuinely new pieces** (competitor map, risk assessment, a phase-mapping of `orchestrator.types.EXECUTION_ORDER`'s 5 real stages) — never a second, competing blueprint generator.

## What was built

**`competitor_map(niche)`** — cites `opportunity_pipeline.annotate_decision()`'s real `competition` field + `value_engine`'s real `competitive_moat` Strategic Investment Layer dimension.

**`risk_assessment(niche)`** — cites `value_engine.compute_value_profile()`'s real `at_risk` flag verbatim.

**`execution_phases()`** — maps `orchestrator.types.EXECUTION_ORDER`'s 5 real stages (`market_intelligence`/`decision`/`production`/`publishing`/`learning`) onto the directive's phase shape. Each phase's `objectives`/`tasks`/`dependencies`/`success_metrics` is a real, deterministic citation of what that stage's own real modules actually do (e.g. production's tasks cite `inspectors.py`'s real Dual Inspection; publishing's tasks cite `distributor.py::distribute`) — same "deterministic template, zero fabricated narrative" discipline `business_dossier.py` already established, never an invented business task list this factory doesn't track. Each `rollback_plan` cites a real, already-existing mechanism (production→`inspectors.py`'s real quarantine path; publishing→`channels/publish_protection.py::trigger_emergency_stop()`; learning→the real append-only nature of `data/decision_outcomes.jsonl`). `real_engine_health` per phase cites `executive_intelligence.engine_health.compute_engine_health()`, already built. `required_ai_models` is cited once, company-wide (`ai_capability.orchestrator.resource_allocation_status()`, the same real source `production_blueprint.py`'s own `required_ai_models` field already uses) — explicitly disclosed as not yet broken down per pipeline stage, rather than fabricating a false stage-to-agent mapping (the 6 named `AGENT_PROMPTS` personas in `server.js` are separate, manually-triggered UI features, confirmed NOT wired into the automated `EXECUTION_ORDER` pipeline — mapping them onto phases would have been a real, disclosed fabrication risk, deliberately avoided).

**`business_blueprint(niche)`** — the aggregator. Calls `production_blueprint.build_production_blueprint()` + `value_engine.compute_value_profile()` + `capital_allocation_engine.investment_score()` exactly once each. One small, purely additive change was needed in `production_blueprint.py`: `expansion_strategy` (already computed internally via `business_dossier`, previously not re-exposed) is now returned as an additional top-level key — avoiding a second, redundant `opportunity_pipeline.annotate_decision()` call, the same real efficiency lesson found and fixed twice already this session (Strategic Intelligence Core, Capital Allocation Engine). `competitor_map()`/`risk_assessment()` both gained an optional `profile=None` injection parameter for the same reason — `business_blueprint()` computes `compute_value_profile()` exactly once and threads it through both, confirmed via a dedicated regression test (`test_never_recomputes_value_profile_for_competitor_map_or_risk`). Returns `None` (never fabricated) when the niche has no real decision on record, matching both underlying blueprints' own convention.

**`business_pipeline_summary()`** — a thin citation of `production_blueprint.build_production_missions_board()` verbatim, reframed under ABB's own naming.

## Mission Control

`mission_control_api.py` gained `business_blueprint`/`business_pipeline_summary`/`execution_phases` endpoints. `server.js`'s `SERVICE_REGISTRY` gained `business-pipeline` (measured live ~11s) and `execution-phases` (measured live ~2s) — both comfortably within the shared default timeout, no disclosed-outlier override needed. `ACTION_REGISTRY` gained `build-business-blueprint` (niche-scoped, 15-minute timeout, same precedent as `investment-score`/`convene-galaxy-council`) + a button in `mission_control.html`'s Opportunities tab. `mission_control_executive_v1.html` gained both no-input panels. Investment Required/Expected Return/Priority/Risk/Confidence (the directive's own named dashboard fields) are direct citations of `investment_score()`'s/`value_engine`'s already-real fields, folded into the per-niche Business Blueprint result rather than a separate new computation.

## Constitution-first / no-autonomous-high-risk boundary (deliberately unchanged)

Every function in `autonomous_business_builder.py` is read-only and recommend-only — no code path in this module publishes anything, spends money, or creates any legal commitment. This matches the directive's own explicit closing constraints and this factory's universal recommend-only discipline, already real everywhere else an irreversible action exists, confirmed unchanged by every prior round this session.

## Validation

New tests: `tests/test_autonomous_business_builder.py` (new, 18 — `competitor_map()`/`risk_assessment()`'s real-signal and honest-Unknown paths including the `profile`-injection guarantee, `execution_phases()`'s real 5-stage ordering/engine-health citation/rollback-plan presence, `business_blueprint()`'s 12-section/8-estimate completeness, the 4-honestly-Unknown-estimates guarantee, the `expansion_strategy` field citation, the ROI real-shape-passthrough guarantee, and the never-recompute-`compute_value_profile()`-twice guarantee, every real source mocked), `tests/test_mission_control_api.py` (+4, the new dispatch endpoints). `tests/test_production_blueprint.py`'s existing 14 tests re-run clean after the additive `expansion_strategy` field change.

Live E2E against real factory data: `business_blueprint()` run against a real ACCEPTED niche ("workflow automation system for logistics companies") took ~16s and produced all 12 real sections and all 8 real estimates (4 real values including a real `development_effort` of $0.0001 over 141 real logged Groq calls, and 4 honestly `Unknown` — monthly/yearly revenue, break-even time, market durability — exactly as predicted by the pre-build audit). `execution_phases()` correctly cited real per-stage engine health (e.g. `market_intelligence` stage: 1346 real total executions, 100% real success rate) from `data/orchestrator_timeline.jsonl`. The full `build-business-blueprint` action was verified live end-to-end through a real, logged-in `server.js` HTTP round-trip (POST → async job → poll → `completed` with the real 12-section/8-estimate result) before declaring the wiring done.

## What's still honestly Unknown / not built

Expected monthly/yearly revenue, break-even time, and market durability will report `Unknown` for every real opportunity for the foreseeable future — this ADR is the record of that being the honest, correct output for a factory with near-zero real sales history, not a gap to "finish later" by inventing a proxy. Per-phase AI-agent/model breakdown stays company-wide only — this factory does not yet track which specific AI capability request belongs to which `EXECUTION_ORDER` stage; building that link is a real, narrow, separately-scoped future improvement, not attempted here to avoid a fabricated stage-to-agent claim.
