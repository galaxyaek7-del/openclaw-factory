**Status:** Permanent, part of the Company DNA (`COMPANY_DNA.md`). The Digital Asset Strategy's 6 named scoring dimensions (Strategic Value, Commercial Value, Long-term Value, Scalability, Automation Potential, Acquisition Potential), checked against real, already-built scoring systems — applied here **only to this company's own real, already-owned assets**, never to hypothetical acquisition targets (`ACQUISITION_ENGINE.md`'s framework-only scope applies to this document too).

## The 6 requested dimensions, mapped to real scoring

| Requested dimension | Real coverage |
|---|---|
| Strategic Value | `strategic_intelligence_core.strategic_score()`'s `strategic_value` dimension |
| Commercial Value | `commercial_readiness.py`'s 6-dimension score (ADR-181) |
| Long-term Value | `strategic_intelligence_core.strategic_score()`'s `long_term_value` dimension + `profit_oracle.py`'s Long-Term Strategic Asset hard gate |
| Scalability | `goos.py`'s `scalability` dimension — honestly `NOT_MEASURABLE_PRE_ACCEPTANCE` for anything not yet a real ACCEPTED opportunity |
| Automation Potential | `strategic_intelligence_core.strategic_score()`'s `automation` dimension |
| Acquisition Potential | **Framework-only, per this round's explicit founder decision** — see below |

**5 of 6 dimensions have real, already-built scoring. The 6th is deliberately not scored for real assets either, in this round, to keep this document consistent with `ACQUISITION_ENGINE.md`'s own restraint.**

## Why Acquisition Potential is not scored here

Scoring this company's own real assets on "how acquirable are we" would be a strange, premature question for a pre-revenue company to ask about itself, and scoring hypothetical *targets* on the same axis is exactly the real-money-adjacent activity `ACQUISITION_ENGINE.md` deliberately holds inactive. Both directions are skipped, honestly, rather than one being quietly answered while the other is refused.

## A real Empire Score, computed honestly, for this company's one real asset

The EU AI Act Compliance Toolkit — the one real, shipped product — scored on the 5 real dimensions: Strategic Value and Long-term Value real but modest (a single-purchase compliance guide, not yet a recurring line); Commercial Value citing `commercial_readiness.py`'s real, current 6-dimension score (bottleneck: financial, `$0` real revenue); Scalability honestly `NOT_MEASURABLE_PRE_ACCEPTANCE`. **A real, honest, unimpressive-sounding score for a company at Stage 1 of 6 — which is the correct, evidence-backed answer, not a discouraging one to soften.**
