# OpenClaw / Galaxy Forge — Acquisition Engine

**Status:** Permanent, part of the Company DNA (`COMPANY_DNA.md`). **Framework only. Zero real capability. Zero code.** This document exists to record the architecture honestly, the same way GCID (ADR-148) and the Global Affiliate Commerce Engine's original 20-network scope (ADR-152) were documented without being built — not because the idea is bad, but because building real acquisition-search infrastructure for a company with $0 revenue and no confirmed legal entity would repeat a mistake this company has already made and corrected twice.

---

## Why this is framework-only, stated directly

Flagged before any writing began: acquiring a business, investing in one, or merging with one requires real money and real legal contracts. This is not a capability gap that more code closes — it is a category of action that requires a real human, a real bank account, a real lawyer, and (per this AI's own standing behavioral rules, independent of any instruction) is not something that gets executed autonomously, ever, regardless of override. **The founder's explicit decision this round: document the categories honestly, keep them inactive, gate them behind the same real conditions as GCID.**

## The Golden Rule condition, checked live before writing this document

Verified directly, not assumed: `finance_data.json`'s only sale record is a labeled smoke test; `data/decisions.jsonl` shows 0 real ACCEPTED opportunities; no real business entity is confirmed anywhere in this repository (the World-Class Readiness Audit's own finding, unchanged since). **The same condition that has blocked every premature-expansion directive this company has received — unchanged as of this writing.**

## The 9 named target categories (`ACQUISITION_ENGINE`'s requested scope) — real status

Small businesses, digital products, communities, websites, domains, AI tools, open-source projects, newsletters, micro SaaS — **none of these has ever had a real candidate evaluated, scored, or contacted by this company.** No search has been run. No target list exists, real or hypothetical, because generating one — even as a hypothetical exercise — risks being mistaken for real evaluation output the moment this document is read out of context. This document intentionally contains zero example targets.

## Re-trigger conditions — the same discipline as GCID (ADR-148)

This document is re-opened for real, active development only when:

(a) A real first dollar exists (checked via `config/reality.json`'s real, unfakeable ledger — never `finance_data.json`'s raw, gameable numbers), **and**
(b) A real, confirmed legal entity exists with real banking and real legal counsel available for due diligence, **and**
(c) The founder explicitly authorizes this specific initiative — the same override pattern already used once, for Affiliate Commerce (ADR-149), never assumed to carry over automatically from that decision to this one.

**All three, not any one.** Acquisitions carry real legal and financial risk in a way a marketing partnership does not — the bar for reopening this document is deliberately higher than the bar that reopened Affiliate Commerce.

## Phase 28 disambiguation (2026-08-08, ADR-218)

**A real naming collision, resolved by disclosure, not by renaming either document.** This filename ("Acquisition Engine") was already claimed by the document above — business/asset **acquisition** (M&A), deliberately kept framework-only per the founder's own standing decision. The "GLOBAL GROWTH & CUSTOMER ACQUISITION ENGINE" directive's Section 2 also names an "Acquisition Engine," but means **customer** acquisition — a completely different, real, already-built concept. That real content lives in `global_growth_engine.py::customer_acquisition_engine()` (reuses `commercial_acquisition.py`'s real 9-channel report directly) and is documented in `CAC_ENGINE.md`/`LTV_ENGINE.md`/`GLOBAL_GROWTH_ENGINE.md` — never merged into this document, since the two "acquisition" concepts (buying a business vs. acquiring a customer) share only a word, not a real system.
