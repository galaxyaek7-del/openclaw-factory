Measure three things before trusting any converter recommendation (learned
the hard way watching regret threads):

1. **Your desk depth.** Under ~28 inches deep, most converters eat half the
   surface when sitting — measure first, then check the unit's sitting
   footprint, not just its standing size.
2. **Monitor weight + height.** A 27"+ monitor on a gas-spring arm behaves
   very differently from a laptop. Wobble complaints cluster at max height
   with heavy monitors — look for reviews that name YOUR setup.
3. **Lift effort with load.** Manual reverse-X mechanisms get hard past ~25
   lbs of gear. If that sounds like your desk, prioritize electric or
   counterbalance, or skip converters for a full desk.

Budget brackets seen repeatedly: ~$150–200 means used or entry-level (check
returns policy); ~$300+ is where stable new units start. Vari = stable but
bulky and priced near full desks; FlexiSpot = compact/budget but verify the
exact model (wobble varies by model, not brand).
