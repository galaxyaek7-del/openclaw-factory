Title: The Etsy Appeal Letters That Actually Get Read (and the 6 Mistakes That Kill Them)

I spent this year studying how Etsy suspension appeals work — not theory, the actual
Appeals Center process. Here is what reviewers respond to, condensed.

**The 4-part structure (200-300 words):**
1. Acknowledgment — exact violation + listing, no excuses.
2. Accountability — plain-language ownership.
3. Action — dated corrections with counts ("removed 14 listings on [date]").
4. Assurance — the permanent system preventing recurrence.

**The 6 killers:** re-filing unchanged appeals; emotional accusations; vague responsibility;
fabricated evidence (instant fraud record); second accounts (evasion pattern); arguing instead
of fixing. Plus the quiet seventh: believing anyone who guarantees reinstatement — no one can.
