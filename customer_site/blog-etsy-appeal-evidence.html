What Evidence Etsy Wants to See in an Appeal

Telling Etsy you fixed the problem is worth little. Showing dated proof is worth everything.
Note: procedure information — not legal advice. Never fabricate evidence: forged documents end appeals instantly and can create a fraud record. Galaxy Forge also sells a $29 appeal kit; this article is complete on its own.

Gather this before writing

  • Dated screenshots of removed listings, fixed descriptions, and updated shop policies — with visible dates.
  • Purchase receipts and commercial licenses for any third-party assets (fonts, graphics, designs), showing the clause that permits commercial use.
  • Supplier invoices for vintage or supply items (redact prices if you like; keep names, dates, descriptions).
  • Creation proof for handmade/digital disputes: workspace photos, raw materials, unmerged design files, or a short creation timelapse.
  • Rights-holder correspondence for IP cases: your acknowledgment plus any retraction response.
  • Payment and case records: cleared balances, resolved case IDs, verification submissions — each dated.

Skip this

Finished-product photos alone (Etsy says explicitly these prove nothing). Emotional character references. Other sellers' listings as comparisons. Promises without dated proof. Anything you cannot defend if asked for the original.

Package it once

One organized set of files, each named clearly, referenced by filename in the letter body. Reviewers process many appeals daily — a tidy evidence pack that matches the letter's claims line by line is the fastest thing a seller can do for their own case.
