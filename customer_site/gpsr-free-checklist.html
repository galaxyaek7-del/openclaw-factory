GPSR Compliance Checklist

For non-EU sellers (US, UK, elsewhere) offering consumer products to EU/EEA buyers on Amazon EU, Etsy, eBay EU or Shopify. Free.

Read first: informational self-help summary only — not legal advice, no attorney-client relationship, no outcome guaranteed. GPSR is Regulation (EU) 2023/988, mandatory since 13 December 2024. Fees, marketplace fields and adjacent rules change — verify every figure and field against current official sources before acting. For cosmetics, toys, electronics, machinery, or any live enforcement dispute, consult a qualified professional.

A. Confirm you are in scope

  • You are established outside the EU, you sell consumer products, and your buyers are in the EU/EEA.
  • There is no handmade exemption and no small-business exemption for GPSR scope.
  • EU-established businesses use a different route — this checklist is not for them.

B. The three documents that unblock most problems

  • An EU-established Responsible Person (name, postal address, electronic contact) designated before the product goes on the EU market.
  • A technical file + risk assessment per product line, kept for ten years after placing on the market.
  • Manufacturer and Responsible Person identity/contact, product identification, and applicable warnings shown on the product/packaging and in the online offer, in a language buyers understand.

C. Practical execution reminders

  • Copy-paste Responsible Person data from the mandate documents — never retype from memory.
  • Keep identifiers identical across listing, label and technical file.
  • Fill the marketplace EU-compliance fields deliberately (trader status, manufacturer, Responsible Person, identifiers, safety text).
  • If de-listed: fix the underlying gap first, then relist with the full data set — do not relist identical non-compliant offers.
  • Start a complaint/incident log now, even if empty — authorities look for the system, not only the papers.
  • On a warning email: reply per cited item with the corrected value, the source document behind it, and a completion date.

D. Verify before you rely on this alone

  • Regulated categories (cosmetics, toys, electronics, machinery): get qualified advice — some have separate regimes on top of GPSR.
  • Products needing lab testing (EMC, toy safety, button batteries): a checklist cannot replace a required test report.
  • Edge cases (vintage, print-on-demand, digital): assess per product; assume no blanket exemption in either direction.
  • All fee figures are dated observations, not quotes — get current terms in writing.
