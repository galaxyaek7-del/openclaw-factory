Temporary vs Permanent Etsy Suspension: Which Path Is Yours?

One line in Etsy's email decides your entire strategy. Read it before doing anything else.
Note: procedure information from Etsy's official documentation — not legal advice. Galaxy Forge also sells a $29 appeal kit; this article is complete on its own.

The one-line test

If the subject line and body say "permanently suspended," you are on the formal appeal path: fix first, then file once via the Appeals Center within 6 months. Anything else — overdue bill, missing tax ID, unfinished bank or identity verification, open cases — is usually a fix-directly situation resolved in days by paying, submitting, or replying to the email.

SituationTypeWhat to do
Overdue billing balanceTemporaryPay the balance; reinstatement is typically automatic
Missing tax ID / identity checkTemporarySubmit documents; reply to the email thread
Unfinished bank verificationTemporaryComplete verification within its time limit
Policy violation (listings/content)Often permanentFix everything first, then formal appeal
IP complaintOften permanentTwo-track: Etsy appeal + rights-holder retraction
"Permanently suspended" emailPermanentAppeals Center, once, with evidence

Why the distinction matters

Filing a formal appeal for a temporary issue wastes reviewers' time and your credibility. Replying to an email thread for a permanent suspension goes nowhere — only the Appeals Center route counts. Sellers who misread this line lose weeks on the wrong path; sellers who read it start correctly on day one.
