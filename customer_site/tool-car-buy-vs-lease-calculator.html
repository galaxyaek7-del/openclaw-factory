Car Buy vs Lease Calculator: Total Cost Over the Same Period

Compare buying with a loan and leasing on the same footing: everything you pay over the lease period, minus what the car is worth to you at the end.

Dealers sell cars on the monthly payment, and a lease payment is almost always lower than a loan payment. That comparison is misleading: at the end of a lease you own nothing, while a buyer owns a car that is still worth money and has paid down part of the loan.

This workbook compares the two over the same period, the length of the lease. For buying it counts the down payment, the loan payments, the loan balance still owed at the end, maintenance, minus the resale value you expect; for leasing it counts the amount due at signing, the payments with tax, the fees and any extra-mileage charge.

It also calculates the resale value at which the two options cost the same, so you can see how much the decision depends on that one estimate.

What is included

  • Buy and lease compared over the same period, not by monthly payment
  • Buying counts the loan balance still owed and the resale value at the end
  • Leasing counts amount due at signing, tax on payments, fees and extra-mileage charges
  • Break-even resale value: how much the decision depends on your resale estimate
  • Works for personal and company-car decisions
  • PDF guide with a worked example and a field-by-field reference

Who it is for

  • Drivers choosing between a lease offer and a car loan
  • Small business owners comparing a company car lease with a purchase
  • Anyone who wants to check a dealer's 'lower monthly payment' pitch

How it works

1) On Inputs, enter the purchase price, sales tax, down payment, loan rate and term from the purchase quote. 2) Enter your estimate of the car's resale value at the end of the lease period (check used prices for the same model at that age and mileage). 3) Enter the lease offer: amount due at signing, monthly payment, months, tax on payments, fees, mileage allowance and your expected miles. 4) Read Comparison: total cost of each option over the period, the difference, and the break-even resale value.

Limits

  • Insurance, fuel and registration are assumed the same for both options and left out.
  • Lease rules (money factor, residual, gap cover) differ by contract; this tool uses the payment and charges you enter.
  • Business tax deductions for vehicles are not modelled.
