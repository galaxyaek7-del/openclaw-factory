Founder Access Continuity Organizer

A one-person business has one single point of failure: you.

If you are in hospital, offline for a week, or simply unreachable, the business does not pause — it fails quietly, because nobody else knows what it runs on or how to get back in. This is the organizer that fixes that, without ever writing a password on paper.

How to use

List every system the business runs on, name a second person who can reach each one, record the recovery route, then route the actual secrets through your password manager's emergency access — never onto paper. Work the Dashboard down to a single readiness verdict, and re-run the drill once a year.
