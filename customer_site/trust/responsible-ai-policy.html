Responsible AI Policy

DRAFT TEMPLATE — NOT LEGALLY REVIEWED. Not legal advice; not approved by a lawyer or regulator.

1. Where AI assists

AI assists parts of our research, content, and product-building process. It helps us move faster; it does not replace the human behind every real decision that matters.

2. Where it deliberately doesn't

We do not use AI to fabricate reviews, testimonials, earnings, customer counts, or partnership claims. If evidence is limited, we say so. Our customer replies are generated from curated, human-toned templates — never from a model pretending to be a person, and never making promises we can't keep.

3. Honesty over polish

If we don't know something, we say "we don't know" — not a confident guess. This policy is enforced in code, not just on paper.
