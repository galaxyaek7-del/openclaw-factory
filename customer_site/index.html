Our approach

An evidence-first production doctrine, not a pitch deck.

Most software gets built on assumptions. Ours gets built on documented, citable evidence — and if the evidence isn't there yet, we say so and go get it instead of shipping a guess.

01 — Proof of Payment

Real spend evidence outweighs inferred demand

We require real, cited evidence that people already pay for a version of this problem — a job posting, an agency's rate card, a competitor's pricing page — before "looks promising" ever becomes "let's build it."

02 — Evidence Completeness

"Unknown" is never treated as "no"

When evidence is missing, we say Unknown and go acquire it — real competitor research, real customer-pain signal — before finalizing a rejection. Every opportunity carries a real, disclosed evidence-coverage score.

03 — Ten-Condition Gate

Commercial value, defensibility, and durability — all required

Competition, defensibility, pricing power, AI-leverage, long-term durability, and profit margin are each independently verified. An opportunity that fails even one still gets rejected, regardless of how good the rest looks.
