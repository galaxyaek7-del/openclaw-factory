About

We help you find better solutions — and we build our own too

Galaxy Forge is a global commercial intelligence and solution-discovery company. We evaluate real digital solutions against real evidence, and we build our own AI-powered software when a real, evidenced gap justifies it.

What we do

Two things, connected by the same evidence discipline. First, we help you find and evaluate digital solutions — automation tools, creative software, e-commerce platforms — verified against their own official terms, never ranked by who pays us the most. Second, we build our own AI-powered business software, but only after real market evidence proves it's worth building.

How we make money

Two ways: some of the solutions we recommend pay us a referral commission if you choose them (see our Affiliate Disclosure for exactly how that does and doesn't influence what we show you), and we sell the software products we build ourselves. Neither one changes what we tell you is true.

How we're run

Galaxy Forge is an early-stage, small operation. AI assists with parts of our research, content, and product-building process — see our Responsible AI Policy for exactly where and where it deliberately isn't used. A human is behind every real decision that matters.

What we won't do: claim a legal registration or physical office we haven't confirmed, invent a customer count or team size, or dress up an early-stage business as something bigger than it is. If you want the exact legal/jurisdiction details, they'll be published once confirmed — see our Privacy Policy for the current, honest status.
