Stripe Chargebacks for Digital Sellers: Evidence That Wins

For sellers of ebooks, templates, software, and courses. Free method below; adapt it to your stack.

1. Log what proves delivery

Account creation timestamps, login records, download logs, license-key activations, email delivery receipts. Export monthly — after a dispute starts, it is too late to reconstruct.

2. Prevent most chargebacks outright

Clear product descriptions (no surprise renewals), delivery confirmation emails, visible refund terms, and basic fraud screening (mismatched countries, disposable emails, rushed bulk orders). Prevention is cheaper than any representment.

3. Build the representment packet

  1. Order details: amount, date, product, buyer identifier.
  2. Delivery proof: the logs from step 1, tied to this order.
  3. Terms accepted: the refund/terms text live at purchase time.
  4. Timeline: one page, calm, factual, complete.

4. Write like an auditor, not a victim

One strong packet beats five angry paragraphs. State what happened, attach the proof, reference the terms. Let the evidence carry the emotion.
