Tiered Sales Commission Calculator for Small Sales Teams

Set up to four commission tiers and a quota bonus, enter each rep's base pay, quota and sales, and see commission paid band by band, quota attainment, total pay and what the plan costs as a share of sales.

Tiered (graduated) commission plans pay a higher rate on each band of sales above a threshold: for example 5% on the first $20,000, 8% on the next $30,000, and so on. They reward top performers without paying the top rate on every dollar, but they are easy to calculate wrongly by hand, and the most common mistake is applying the highest reached rate to all sales.

This calculator applies each rate only to the sales inside its band, then adds an optional flat bonus for reps who reach quota. For every rep it shows quota attainment, commission, bonus, total pay, the effective commission rate and total pay as a share of their sales. The summary shows team totals, how many reps hit quota and what the whole plan costs as a percentage of sales.

Use it to pay commission each month and, before you change a plan, to see what the new tiers would have paid last quarter.

How it works

1) On Plan, enter the four tier thresholds (the first is always 0) and the rate for each tier, plus any quota bonus. 2) On Reps, list each rep with base pay, quota and sales for the period. 3) Read each rep's commission and total pay, and Summary for the team.

Limits

  • Four tiers and one flat quota bonus; clawbacks, draws, splits and per-product rates are not modelled.
  • Up to 50 reps per period; use one copy per month or quarter.
  • Tax and payroll deductions are not calculated.
