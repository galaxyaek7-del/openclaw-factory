How to choose a business solution without getting fooled by marketing

This is how Galaxy Forge actually evaluates the solutions listed on this site — and it works just as well if you use it yourself on a tool we haven't covered yet.

1. Separate the vendor's claim from independent evidence

Any vendor can say their product is "the best." What matters is whether that claim is backed by something you can verify independently — the vendor's own official terms page, third-party analysis, or a real feature you can test yourself. Galaxy Forge only lists a solution once we've confirmed its terms directly against its own official domain — never based on a blog post or a paid review.

2. Know what "verified" actually means (and doesn't)

On this site, a solution's real verification status tells you how strong the evidence behind it is:

  • Verified: confirmed directly against the vendor's own official page.
  • Provisional: real evidence exists but hasn't cleared every check yet.

We deliberately do not show solutions whose only evidence is a third-party claim, or whose data has gone stale. That's not the same as saying every "verified" solution is right for you — it means the facts we show you about it are real.

3. Ask what the vendor's page doesn't tell you

Every product page is written to sell. Before deciding, ask: What does this NOT do well? Who has outgrown it? What's the real cost after the introductory period ends? Vendor pages rarely answer these directly — comparison and community discussion (forums, review sites with real user history) usually do.

4. Never let commission or sponsorship decide for you

If a site's top pick always happens to be whatever pays the highest referral fee, that's a real conflict of interest, whether or not it's disclosed. Galaxy Forge's own ranking never uses commission value as an input — see our Affiliate Disclosure for exactly how we handle this.

An honest limitation of this guide: Galaxy Forge does not yet track detailed feature-by-feature or pricing comparisons for the solutions in our catalog — that's real, disclosed future work, not something we're pretending to have today. Use the method above on the vendor's own official page for now; we'll expand our own evidence base over time.
