# Galaxy Forge — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 7, 2026

## What Data We Collect

### When you purchase a product:
- **Name** (as provided at checkout)
- **Email address** (for order confirmation and delivery)
- **Payment information** (processed by Paddle/Gumroad — we do not store it)
- **Order details** (product purchased, price, date)

### When you visit our site:
- **Basic analytics** (page views, referrer) — if analytics are enabled
- **No cookies** beyond what's required for site functionality

### We do NOT collect:
- Social security numbers
- Credit card numbers (processed by payment platforms)
- Browsing history on other sites
- Location data beyond country-level

## How We Use Your Data

| Purpose | Data Used | Legal Basis |
|---------|-----------|-------------|
| Order fulfillment | Name, email | Contract performance |
| Customer support | Name, email, order details | Legitimate interest |
| Product improvement | Aggregated purchase data | Legitimate interest |
| Legal compliance | Order records | Legal obligation |

## Data Sharing

We share data with:
- **Payment processors** (Paddle, Gumroad) — for payment processing only
- **Email service providers** — for order confirmations (when configured)

We do NOT:
- Sell your data to third parties
- Share your data with advertisers
- Use your data for targeted advertising

## Data Retention

- **Order records:** Retained indefinitely for accounting and support purposes
- **Support communications:** Retained for 2 years after last contact
- **Analytics data:** Aggregated and anonymized
