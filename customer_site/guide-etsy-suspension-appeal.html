1. Triage: temporary or permanent?

Check the subject line of Etsy's email. "Permanently suspended" means the formal appeal path. Anything else — overdue bill, missing tax ID, unfinished bank verification, open cases — is usually fixed directly: pay, submit, reply to the email, and the shop typically reopens within days. Filing a formal appeal for a temporary issue wastes your one shot at being taken seriously.

2. Fix first, write second

The appeal's job is to prove the problem is resolved, not to argue it never happened. Before writing: remove every listing related to the cited violation, resolve open cases, pay balances, and save evidence (screenshots with dates, removed-listing lists, updated policies). Etsy's Appeals Center asks what actions you took — "I fixed it, here is proof" beats "I don't know why I was suspended" every time.

3. File exactly once, in the right place

Permanent suspensions go through etsy.com/appeals, signed in with the suspended account, within 6 months of the suspension date — miss the window and the decision is final. Temporary issues go through replying to the suspension email. Do not email generic support, do not open a second account (linked-account closure converts a contestable record into an evasion pattern), and do not re-file the same appeal — reviewers have seen every template.

4. The 4-part letter that reviewers actually read

  1. Acknowledgment — name the exact violation and listing, factually, no excuses.
  2. Accountability — your role in it, in plain language ("I did not know X violated policy Y, I now understand why").
  3. Action taken — concrete corrections with specifics ("removed 14 related listings on [date]").
  4. Assurance — the lasting system preventing recurrence (a compliance checklist in your listing process, trademark screening before publishing).

5. IP complaints are two-track

If a rights holder filed the complaint, Etsy largely processes the notice — the appeal must also go to the claimant. Contact the brand's IP team directly, acknowledge, confirm removal, and ask for a retraction before or alongside the Etsy appeal. A retraction letter changes the odds more than any wording tweak.

6. Wait properly

Decisions take up to two weeks by email. One appeal, then silence — no follow-up spam, no duplicate filings. A denial after a permanent-suspension appeal is final; at that point the honest options are senior-case-manager review request, counsel for high-stakes shops, or rebuilding on another platform — not a third copy of the same letter.
