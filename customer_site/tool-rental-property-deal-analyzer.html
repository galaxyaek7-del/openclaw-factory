Rental Property Deal Analyzer

Compare up to 25 buy-and-hold rental deals side by side: monthly cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash return, DSCR and a clear verdict against your own targets.

Most rental deals that lose money looked fine on a back-of-the-envelope calculation: rent minus mortgage. The costs that sink them are the ones that arrive later and irregularly: vacancy, repairs, capital expenses, management.

This analyzer puts every deal through the same full calculation: financing, operating costs, reserves for vacancy, maintenance and capital expenses, then net operating income, monthly cash flow and the standard return measures lenders and investors use, so you can compare deals on the same terms.

Each property is one row, so you can line up 25 candidates and see in seconds which ones meet your targets and which only work if nothing goes wrong.

What is included

  • One row per property, up to 25 deals compared side by side
  • Mortgage payment (PMT), NOI, monthly cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash return, DSCR and rent-to-price
  • Vacancy, maintenance, management and CapEx reserves built in, so the numbers are not best-case
  • Verdict per deal against the cash-on-cash and DSCR targets you set
  • Handles cash purchases (100% down) as well as financed deals
  • PDF guide with three worked example deals and a field-by-field reference

Who it is for

  • First-time and small portfolio rental investors
  • House hackers comparing a duplex against a single-family home
  • Agents and wholesalers presenting numbers to buyers
  • Anyone checking a seller's or agent's pro forma

How it works

1) On Targets, set the minimum cash-on-cash return and DSCR you require (8% and 1.25 are common starting points). 2) On Deals, replace the three example properties with your own: price, closing costs, rehab, financing, rent and costs. Use annual figures where the column says annual, monthly where it says monthly, and percentages as percentages. 3) Read the grey columns: cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash, DSCR and the verdict. 4) Change one input at a time (rent, rate, down payment) to see what the deal needs to work. 5) The Summary shows how many deals meet your targets and the best return in the list.

Limits

  • Fixed-rate amortising loans only; no interest-only periods, ARMs or private-money balloons.
  • Year-one view: no appreciation, rent growth, tax benefits or principal paydown are included.
  • Taxes and insurance vary widely by location; use real quotes.
