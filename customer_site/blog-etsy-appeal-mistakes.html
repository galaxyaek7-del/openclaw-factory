7 Etsy Appeal Mistakes That Get Appeals Rejected

Each of these is observed repeatedly in seller reports. Avoid all seven and the appeal is already above average.
Note: procedure information — not legal advice. Galaxy Forge also sells a $29 appeal kit; this article is complete on its own.

1. Re-filing the same appeal

Filing again with no new information signals the seller isn't taking the process seriously. Re-appeal only with genuinely new evidence or documentation.

2. Emotional accusations

Claims of unfairness, comparisons to other sellers, and legal threats read as deflection. Reviewers respond to facts and corrective action, not pressure.

3. Vague responsibility

"I didn't do anything wrong" addresses nothing. Even a disputed suspension must engage the cited violation specifically, with dates and listing names.

4. Fabricated evidence

Forged invoices, edited screenshots, or invented licenses end the appeal instantly when detected — and can convert a contestable case into a fraud record. Never fabricate anything.

5. Opening a second account

Etsy links accounts through devices, IPs, payment instruments, and addresses. A new account after suspension is typically closed as a linked account — and converts a contestable record into demonstrated evasion, which violates Etsy's policies. Appeal the original shop instead.

6. Arguing instead of fixing

Long explanations of why the rule is wrong score zero. Dated corrections plus a prevention system score everything. Reviewers ask one question: is the problem resolved?

7. Believing anyone who guarantees reinstatement

No seller, consultant, or kit — including ours — can guarantee what Etsy's reviewers decide. Anyone promising a guaranteed outcome is selling something other than honesty. Our own kit states this on every page.
