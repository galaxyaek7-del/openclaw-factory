Free Pilot: We Clean Up Your Messy Spreadsheet and Show Exactly What Changed

Contact lists, inventory exports and CRM downloads fill up with duplicates, mixed date formats and half-empty rows. We are running a free pilot for a handful of small teams: you describe the file, we return a clean one together with a report that shows exactly what changed.

Disclosure: this is a manual service run by a small team, not software you log into. The pilot is free; in return we ask for one honest feedback answer afterwards. We make no promise about outcomes beyond the report we send, and we will say so before starting if your file is outside what we can do. Not bookkeeping, tax or financial advice.

What you get

  • A cleaned CSV or Excel file: consistent column names, standardised dates and fields, duplicates identified and removed.
  • A QA report: rows in and out, columns mapped, duplicates removed, values flagged as missing, and anything we were unsure about and left for you to decide.
  • Delivery by email, normally within five business days after we confirm the scope.

What we need from you

  1. A short description first: the kind of file, roughly how many rows, which columns matter (dates, currency, SKUs, emails) and the problems you already know about. Please do not send the file yet.
  2. We reply within two business days to confirm the scope, or to decline honestly if it is outside what we can do. Only then do you send the file.
  3. Send nothing you are not allowed to share. Files over about 50,000 rows are outside the pilot.
Not included: bookkeeping judgement, tax or financial advice, writing into your databases or any system that needs your login, and fixing the source system that produces the mess.

Why a pilot

We have tested the cleaning process on sample data of our own, not yet on a real customer's file. We would rather learn on a few real files, openly, than sell something we have not proven. If the pilot works we will publish a price; until then there is none.
