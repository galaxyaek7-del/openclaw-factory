Home Renovation Budget & Contractor Quote Comparison

Compare contractor quotes line by line, catch the quote that leaves items out, choose per item, and see your total with a contingency against the budget.

Renovation quotes are hard to compare. One contractor bundles demolition into the job, another leaves out disposal, a third quotes fixtures as 'client supplied'. The lowest total is often the quote that covers least.

This workbook breaks the job into line items. You enter your own estimate and each contractor's price per item; it shows the lowest price per item, totals each contractor only over the items they actually priced, and marks a quote INCOMPLETE when it leaves items out, so totals are never compared unfairly.

You then choose who does each item. The Summary adds a contingency you set, compares the total with your budget, and shows how much room is left.

What is included

  • Line-by-line comparison of three contractor quotes with your own estimate
  • Lowest price per item and contractor totals over the items they actually priced
  • INCOMPLETE flag for any quote that leaves items out
  • Choose a contractor per item; contingency added at the rate you set
  • Budget check: total with contingency against your budget
  • PDF guide with a worked example and a field-by-field reference

Who it is for

  • Homeowners planning a kitchen, bathroom or whole-house renovation
  • Landlords comparing quotes for a unit refurbishment
  • Anyone collecting three quotes and wanting a fair comparison

How it works

1) On Project, enter your budget and the contingency you want to keep (the example uses 15%). 2) On Line Items, list the work item by item and add your own estimate for each. 3) Enter each contractor's price per item, leaving a cell empty when they did not price that item. 4) In Chosen, type A, B or C for the contractor you prefer for each item. 5) Read the Summary: totals per contractor, which quotes are incomplete, your chosen total with contingency, and the budget left.

Limits

  • Up to 25 line items and three contractors.
  • Prices are compared as entered; materials grade, warranty and schedule are not scored.
  • Permit fees and design fees should be entered as their own line items.
