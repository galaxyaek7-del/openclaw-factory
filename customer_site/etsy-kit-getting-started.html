Got the kit? Start here (20 minutes)

The exact sequence buyers should follow. Skipping steps is how appeals fail.
  1. Read your suspension notice twice. Highlight the type (temporary vs permanent) and the exact cited reason. Everything below depends on this.
  2. Confirm the kit fits. Check the FAQ fit section — if your case is outside coverage, request a refund within 7 days instead of forcing it.
  3. Work the triage chapter first. Do not jump to the letter templates. Triage decides which path (fix-directly vs formal appeal) your case needs.
  4. Gather evidence before writing. Dated screenshots, receipts, licenses, creation proof — one folder, clearly named files.
  5. Adapt templates truthfully. Fill every bracket with your real facts. Never invent dates, counts, or correspondence. A template with false specifics is worse than no template.
  6. Review against the filing checklist. Shop name, dates, policy cited, evidence references, tone. Then file once via the Appeals Center (permanent) or the email thread (temporary).
  7. Wait properly. Up to two weeks. No duplicate filings, no second account, no spam. One polite follow-up after 14 days of silence, maximum.
