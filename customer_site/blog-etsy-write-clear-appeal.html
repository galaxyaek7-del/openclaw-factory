How to Write a Clear Etsy Suspension Appeal

Reviewers decide on specifics, evidence, and tone. Here is the structure that carries all three in 200–300 words.
Note: procedure information — not legal advice. File through Etsy's Appeals Center for permanent suspensions. Galaxy Forge also sells a $29 appeal kit; this article is complete on its own.

1. Open with the facts, not feelings

Shop name and ID, suspension date, the exact policy cited. One paragraph. Reviewers process many appeals daily — the faster they locate your case, the better.

2. Name the violation precisely

Quote the policy section and identify the triggering listings by name. "I don't know why I was suspended" is not an appeal; even a disputed suspension must address the cited reason head-on.

3. Show corrective action with dates and counts

What you removed, changed, paid, or submitted — each with a date. "Removed 14 related listings on [date]" beats "I fixed everything" completely. Attach the screenshots as evidence.

4. State the prevention system

What changed permanently: a compliance checklist in the listing process, trademark screening before publishing, weekly listing audits. Reviewers look for a future without repeat violations.

5. Close briefly and professionally

One respectful reinstatement request plus an offer of further documentation. No legal threats, no comparisons to other sellers, no accusations of unfairness — each of those actively harms the appeal.

Length rule: 200–300 words total. Simple professional language. Proofread twice. A concise, specific, evidence-backed appeal outperforms a long emotional one every time — this matches Etsy's own guidance to be transparent and forthcoming with details.
