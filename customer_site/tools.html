Spreadsheet tools

Each tool is a working Excel workbook with locked formulas, clearly marked input cells, an example already filled in, and a PDF guide with a worked example. Every formula is checked by recalculating it independently before release.

  • 13-Week Cash Flow Forecast for Small Business ($19)
    See every week's cash position for the next quarter, spot the tight weeks before they happen, and track forecast against actual.
  • Rental Property Deal Analyzer ($24)
    Compare up to 25 buy-and-hold rental deals side by side: monthly cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash return, DSCR and a clear verdict against your own targets.
  • Debt Payoff Planner (Avalanche or Snowball) ($12)
    A real month-by-month payoff schedule for up to six debts: your debt-free month, total interest, and how much you save against paying minimums only.
  • Self-Employed Quarterly Tax Set-Aside Planner (US, 2026) ($12)
    Log income and expenses as they happen and see, each quarter, how much to set aside and pay for federal income tax, self-employment tax and state tax.
  • Etsy & Shopify Product Profit Calculator ($12)
    The real profit on every product after platform fees, payment processing, Offsite Ads, shipping, packaging and ad spend, plus the price you need for the margin you want.
  • Service Business Pricing & Break-Even Calculator ($15)
    Price every service from your real costs and capacity: cost per billable hour, minimum price, target-margin price, and whether next month's plan covers your costs and your own pay.
  • Inventory Reorder Point & Stock Tracker ($15)
    Know what to reorder, when and how much: reorder points with safety stock, days of stock left, and a suggested order list for every SKU.
  • Client Pipeline & Proposal Tracker ($14)
    Track every lead and proposal from first contact to won or lost: weighted pipeline value, win rate, follow-ups due, and which lead sources actually bring paying clients.
  • Startup Runway & Burn Planner ($15)
    Month by month for three years: revenue, costs, burn and cash. See your runway, your break-even month, and whether you reach break-even before the money runs out, with a second scenario to compare.
  • Zero-Based Monthly Budget Planner ($9)
    Give every dollar of this month's income a job, log spending as it happens, and see category by category where the money actually went.
