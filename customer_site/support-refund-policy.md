**Last Updated:** September 7, 2026

## What We Sell

Galaxy Forge sells digital products — PDF toolkits, templates, journals, and automation playbooks. These are downloadable digital goods, not physical products.

## Refund Policy

### Digital Products (PDF Downloads)

**30-day money-back guarantee.** If you're not satisfied with your purchase, contact us within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.

**How to request a refund:**
1. Email us at [support email — TO BE CONFIGURED]
2. Include your order number or the email used at checkout
3. Tell us what didn't meet your expectations

**Refund processing:**
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method
- You will receive an email confirmation when your refund is processed

### What qualifies for a refund:

- Product doesn't match the description
- Product has quality issues (broken PDF, missing content)
- You changed your mind within 30 days (no questions asked)
- Product isn't compatible with your stated use case

### What doesn't qualify for a refund:

- Requests made after 30 days from purchase
- Products downloaded and used but claimed as "not downloaded"
- Requests for physical products (we don't sell any)

## Product Quality Commitment

Every digital product we sell goes through a quality inspection before publication:
- **Technical inspection:** PDF renders correctly, all pages present, no corruption
- **Commercial validation:** Content matches description, pricing is fair
- **Compliance check:** Products include required disclaimers (e.g., "not legal advice" for regulatory toolkits)

If you find a quality issue, we will:
1. Fix the product and provide an updated version free of charge, OR
2. Issue a full refund — your choice
