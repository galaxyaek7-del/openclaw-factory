**Status:** Permanent, part of the Company DNA (`COMPANY_DNA.md`). The directive's 5 self-questioning prompts are checked here against `gfos.py::if_i_were_the_ceo_report()` (7 named questions, real, already weekly) — this company has been asking itself a close variant of these questions since ADR-172, three named-CEO-directive rounds before this one.

## Cadence — real, not aspirational

- **Weekly:** `if_i_were_the_ceo_report()` (ADR-172) + the Galaxy Evolution Report (ADR-187) + the Trust Audit Report (`trust_audit.py`, ADR-189) — all three folded into the same real, Sunday-gated combined export (`mission_control_api.py::_build_combined_executive_report_markdown()`), zero new scheduling.
- **Monthly:** `evolution_engine.py::build_galaxy_evolution_report()`'s own standalone dated report file (`maybeGenerateMonthlyGalaxyEvolutionReport`).
- **Quarterly / Annual:** `enterprise_validation.py`'s real validation report and `strategic_planning.py`'s real roadmap dashboard, on their own real cadence gates (`GALAXY_FORGE_EXECUTIVE_CONSTITUTION.md`, ADR-177).

## "Would the world's best digital company release this?" — a decision lens, not a module

Named directly in the Trust & Excellence Constitution (`trust_audit.py`, ADR-189) and the Company DNA's own quality standard. It is applied by every human (or AI session) making a real publish/approve decision, the same way `COMPANY_DNA.md`'s "would this matter at 100/10,000/1,000,000 customers" test is applied — a real question asked before building, not a score computed by code, because no real benchmark data of "the world's best digital company's actual internal standards" exists anywhere for a mechanical check to cite honestly.

## What self-review has already found, for real

This is not a hypothetical protocol. Applying it produced real, disclosed findings already acted on this session: the World-Class Readiness Audit found the checkout flow itself was the worst real friction point (fixed, ADR-183); the EU AI Act toolkit's own regulatory claim was found stale and corrected before a single sale (`OpenClaw_Brain/19_Lessons_Learned/`); the pricing was found unrealistic under the pipeline's own default tier and corrected downward, not up (ADR-182). Self-review that never changes a real decision is not a real protocol — this company's own history is the evidence that this one does.
