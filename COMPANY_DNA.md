# OpenClaw / Galaxy Forge — Company DNA

**Status:** Permanent. This document is the highest authority inside the organization. Every product, feature, partnership, and AI recommendation is checked against it before execution (see `INTEGRITY_RULES.md`). It supersedes convenience, speed, and short-term revenue whenever they conflict.

**Ratified:** 2026-08-07. Not a founding document written in a vacuum — a distillation of what this company has already proven true about itself across roughly six weeks of real operating history: what it built, what it refused to build, what it got wrong and corrected in public, and what actually moved it closer to a real customer. Every claim below traces to something real, cited inline, not invented for the occasion.

---

## 1. Mission — Why the company exists

**OpenClaw exists to prove, before it builds, and to sell what it proves.**

Not "build digital products." Not "automate a company." The company's actual, hard-won identity is narrower and more specific than either: a discipline for turning real evidence of demand into real, sellable digital assets, and a refusal to let internal sophistication substitute for a stranger's real willingness to pay.

This mission was not declared once and left alone — it was tested and re-confirmed the hard way. The Proof of Payment doctrine (`profit_oracle.py`, ADR-121) has rejected every one of 87 real evaluated niches at least once specifically because evidence of real spend didn't exist yet, not because the ideas were bad. The Truth First Constitution (`truth_first.py`, ADR-160) exists because this company, mid-session, caught itself almost shipping a legal disclaimer that a prompt promised but never rendered, and a price that no page ever actually earned. The long-term impact this company wants to create: a small, evidence-first digital business that other solo builders can point to and say — that one didn't fake its way to the top, and it still won.

## 2. Vision — What OpenClaw should become

Honesty about the starting point is part of the vision, not a disclaimer on it. As of this writing, `growth_stages.py`'s own real, mechanical classifier places this company at **Stage 1 — Validation** of 6 named stages, with $0 real customer revenue and one real, live payment channel still blocked on the founder's own external onboarding. The vision below is a direction, built on the real architecture already in place — not a forecast dressed up as a certainty.

**5 years:** A real, profitable, small digital-goods company with at least one durable recurring-revenue line (the EU AI Act Compliance Toolkit's own regulatory-update subscription model, described in its launch kit, is the first real candidate), a real audience that discovers it without being told to, and a Growth Stage 3–4 position — multiple proven products, at least one real international channel, and a company able to survive a bad month without the founder personally intervening in every transaction.

**10 years:** One of the six tracks named in this repo's own architecture (digital books, templates, digital art, apps/tools, business services, international trade — see the project's own three-layer Core/Engines/Channels model) has grown into a real, standalone business line with its own team, its own customers, and its own P&L — chosen because the evidence said so, not because it was the most exciting idea in a given week. The company by then has a real legal entity, real accounting, and a real answer to every question a professional investor would ask, because those questions were asked and answered honestly along the way (see the World-Class Readiness Audit's own finding: today, several of those answers are honestly "Unknown," not fabricated).

**20 years:** A genuinely autonomous digital enterprise in the narrow, real sense this company has already defined and defended for itself — automation and AI carrying the discovery, evaluation, production, and operational load, with human judgment reserved permanently for exactly four things this company has already, explicitly, repeatedly refused to automate away: capital reallocation, business retirement, evolution-queue execution, and elevated-risk publishing (see `INTEGRITY_RULES.md` §4). Not "no humans." A company where the humans who remain are making the decisions that were always supposed to require a human.

## 3. Non-Negotiable Principles

Each principle below is enforced by real, existing code — named here so "non-negotiable" means something checkable, not aspirational.

1. **Absolute honesty.** Enforced by the Truth First Constitution (`truth_first.py`, ADR-160) — the company's own highest law before this document. No fabricated metric, review, customer, or capability has ever shipped; where evidence doesn't exist, the system says `Unknown`, `DISCOVERY`, or `NOT_MEASURABLE` — never a plausible-sounding guess. `reality_audit.py`'s repeated live audits (98–99% REAL across 166 real endpoints) are how this is checked, not assumed.
2. **Customer success before short-term profit.** Enforced by `brand_dna.py`'s Trust Principles and the Trust & Excellence Constitution (`trust_audit.py`, ADR-189) — a product cannot ship with a fake urgency claim, a deceptive price, or a hidden limitation. The EU AI Act toolkit's own regulatory correction — rewriting the product's central claim after finding it had gone stale, before a single sale had happened — is this principle in its most literal, already-proven form.
3. **Premium quality over quantity.** Enforced by Dual Inspection (`inspectors.py`) and `executive_quality_gate.py`'s `REJECT_IF_FAIL` gate. `QUARANTINE.md`'s own real, 3,300+-entry rejection history is not a record of failure — it is the record of this principle actually being applied, repeatedly, including against this company's own work.
4. **Long-term assets over temporary trends.** Enforced by `profit_oracle.py`'s Long-Term Strategic Asset hard gate (ADR-122/126) and `goos.py`'s `difficulty_of_copying` / `scalability` / `recurring_revenue_potential` dimensions — a real opportunity is scored on whether it compounds, not on whether it's exciting this week.
5. **Evidence over assumptions.** The Proof of Payment doctrine is this principle's sharpest edge: 0 of 87 real evaluated niches have ever been accepted without it, even when that meant staying at $0 revenue for weeks rather than relaxing the bar. This is the one principle this company has paid the most real, measurable cost to keep.
