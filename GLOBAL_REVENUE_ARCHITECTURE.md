**1 of 10 streams is real and active. 3 have real infrastructure with $0 real revenue. 6 are honest, disclosed gaps.** This is not diversification yet — it is one real product, several real but dormant channels, and several genuinely unbuilt categories.

## Why "continuously expand" doesn't mean "build all 10 now"

The Golden Rule (`COMPANY_DNA.md`) applies here directly: no new revenue-stream architecture before the first real dollar from the current one. This document names the real map of where expansion *could* go — it does not authorize building toward all 10 simultaneously, which would repeat the exact premature-scope pattern already declined for GCID (ADR-148) and the original Affiliate Engine scope (ADR-152).

## The real, current sequencing

1. Get the one real product through its one real, live payment channel (Paddle onboarding, founder-only).
2. Prove the subscription concept on the same product, once it has real base sales.
3. Only then does a second stream (Affiliate, Marketplace, Strategic Partnerships — all three already have real code waiting) become the honest next priority, in whichever order real evidence favors.
